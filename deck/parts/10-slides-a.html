03Where students go学生的目的地

Demand moved into the markets we serve需求正流向我们所服务的市场

Enrolment from China to the US is down about 29% from its peak, and US visa policy remains unstable. The UK still receives the largest number of Chinese Masters students. Hong Kong and Singapore now take the extra demand. Most clients now apply to the UK, Hong Kong, and Singapore at the same time.中国赴美留学人数较峰值下降约 29%，美国签证政策仍不稳定。英国仍是接收中国硕士学生最多的国家。香港和新加坡正承接额外的需求。大多数客户现在同时申请英国、香港和新加坡。

04Market supply市场供给

Our price segment lost its leading companies我们所在价格段的领先公司相继出局

再来人 Valeon · 12/2025

This was the leading premium Masters company. It closed in December 2025. It paid mentors ¥30k–50k per student, so its profit margin fell below 30%. It could not pay staff and shut down. Its clients now need a new provider.这曾是高端硕士申请领域的行业标杆，2025 年 12 月倒闭。它为每位学生支付 3–5 万元导师费，利润率跌破 30%。无力支付员工工资，随即停业。它的客户现在需要新的服务提供商。

天道 · 2022

This was a leading graduate-admissions brand in the 2010s. It spent client prepayments as working capital. When it closed, it owed ¥14.23M in unrefunded fees. Chinese families still remember this case.这是 2010 年代领先的硕博申请品牌。它把客户预付款当作运营资金使用。倒闭时，尚欠退费 1423 万元。中国家庭至今记得这个案例。

世毕盟 GGU

This company is still open but has problems. It charges ¥80k–100k in fees but has only about 15 insured staff. A well-known complaint thread on the forum 一亩三分地 appears in its search results.这家公司仍在运营，但存在问题。它收费 8–10 万元，但仅有约 15 名参保员工。一亩三分地论坛上一条知名的投诉长帖，出现在它的搜索结果中。

IDP / ApplyBoard / Adventus

These companies earn money from university commissions. Their value fell more than 90% from their peak, or more than 80% was written off, or they closed. All three depended on the same thing: stable visa policy.这些公司靠院校佣金赚钱。它们的市值较峰值下跌超过 90%，或被减记超过 80%，或已倒闭。三家都依赖同一件事：稳定的签证政策。

Meanwhile, the strongest companies in this market — 指南者, mbaMission, Menlo, and Studyportals — share four traits: they did not take investor funding, they publish clear prices, all staff work full time, and they own their marketing channels. The rest of this deck is built on that pattern.与此同时，这个市场里最强健的公司——指南者、mbaMission、Menlo、Studyportals——共有四个特征：不接受投资人融资、公开定价、全员全职、拥有自己的营销渠道。本汇报后续内容，正是建立在这个规律之上。

05Our position我们的定位

No company offers all six things below. We will.没有一家公司同时做到以下六件事。我们要做到。

We reviewed all 141 companies. None of them combine the six practices below. Each practice exists because it is the direct fix for a documented cause of failure.我们审阅了全部 141 家公司。没有一家同时具备以下六项做法。每一项都是针对某个有据可查的失败原因所做的直接修正。

Published prices公开定价

Every Chinese premium firm hides its prices. Hidden prices are linked to the most common client complaint: the firm reduces service after the client signs.每一家中国高端机构都不公开价格。价格不透明与最常见的客户投诉直接相关：机构在客户签约后降低服务水平。

Senior staff, full time, limited caseload资深顾问 · 全职 · 限制接案量

Networks of part-time mentors damaged GGU and closed Valeon. Full-time staff are the only way we found to keep quality high at a premium price.兼职导师网络拖垮了世毕盟、关闭了再来人。全职员工是我们发现的、在高端价格下维持质量的唯一方法。

Payment in stages, held in a separate account分阶段付款，资金存入独立账户

Spending client prepayments before the work is done is the leading cause of company failure in this market (天道, 太傻). We will make refund safety a selling point.在完成工作前动用客户预付款，是这个市场中公司倒闭的头号原因（天道、太傻）。我们要把「退费安全」做成一个卖点。

A real UK office真实的英国办公室

优越 and UKEC advertise a UK presence but do not deliver UK-based work at a premium level. We can prove ours is real, which they cannot copy.优越和 UKEC 宣传自己在英国有布局，但没有真正提供英国本地的高水准服务。我们可以证明自己的英国布局是真实的，而这一点他们无法复制。

No university commissions不收院校佣金

We take payment only from the client, not from universities. Clients already discuss this conflict of interest openly online. We remove it.我们只收客户的钱，不收大学的钱。客户已经在网上公开讨论这种利益冲突。我们从根本上消除它。

Checked results可核实的成果

Only 2 of the 141 firms have an outside party check their results. Most published numbers in this market are not trusted. A checked number, even a modest one, builds more trust than a large unchecked claim.141 家公司中只有 2 家的成果数据经第三方核实。这个市场里大多数公开数字都不被信任。一个经过核实的普通数字，比一个未经核实的漂亮数字更能建立信任。

This is already running: the founder posts through a four-account, paid-and-organic 小红书 operation today. The next steps: keep all records, contracts, and client data with the company, and serve the UK, Hong Kong, and Singapore, with the US and PhD applications as add-on options.这一项已经在运行：创始人目前已经通过「付费+自然流量」的四账号小红书矩阵发帖。接下来的任务：所有记录、合同和客户数据由公司统一保管，并服务英国、香港、新加坡三地，美国及博士申请作为附加选项。

06Pricing定价

Our price range: ¥40,000 to ¥80,000我们的价格区间：4 万至 8 万元

Masters application price ranges, in RMB thousands硕士申请套餐价格区间（人民币千元）
We use each firm's published price where one exists. Where none exists, we use the range reported on forums. The shaded area is our target price range.凡有公开定价的机构均取其公开价；无公开定价的取论坛引用区间。底纹区为我们的目标价格带。

Below our range, Compass charges ¥18,000 to ¥24,000 and uses junior full-time staff. It cannot offer deep, senior-level service at that price. Above our range, the ¥100,000+ mentor-based firms have closed or lost trust. We price at 2 to 3 times Compass's rate, because we offer senior staff and a limited caseload.低于我们价格带的指南者，收费 1.8–2.4 万元，使用初级全职团队，在这个价位上无法提供资深、深度的服务。高于我们价格带的 10 万元以上导师制机构，已经倒闭或失去信任。我们的定价是指南者的 2–3 倍，理由是我们提供资深顾问，并限制接案量。

Note: our real deals already average ¥73,000 to ¥82,000, at the top of this range. The task is to publish this price band, not invent it.备注：我们目前的真实成交均价已达 7.3–8.2 万元，处于此区间的高端。要做的是把这个价格带公开，而不是重新定价。

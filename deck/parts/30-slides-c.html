15Company rules公司规则

Ten things we will not do十件我们绝不做的事

Each rule below stopped a real company in our research. We write these down as fixed policy.以下每一条规则，在我们的研究中都对应一家真实倒下的公司。我们将其写成固定政策。

We will not spend client prepayments before the work is done. This closed 天道 and 太傻.不动用客户预付款用于未完成的工作。这是天道和太傻倒闭的原因。
We will not take commissions from universities. This causes the conflict of interest seen at 51offer, IDP, and UKEC.不收取院校佣金。这正是 51offer、IDP、UKEC 存在的利益冲突根源。
We will not sell 保录取 or refund guarantees. This created debt at 太傻 and fraud at AdmitWrite.不出售保录取或退款担保。这在太傻造成了负债，在 AdmitWrite 导致了欺诈。
We will not build a network of part-time mentors at a premium price. This is why GGU and Valeon had problems.不在高价位建立兼职导师网络。这正是世毕盟和再来人出问题的原因。
We will not pay staff based on signed contracts alone. Pay is tied to client satisfaction after delivery — the opposite of 金吉列's model.不只按签约数量给员工发薪。薪酬与交付后的客户满意度挂钩——这与金吉列的模式相反。
We will not raise investor funding for the core advisory business. No investor-funded firm in this market became a lasting success.核心咨询业务不接受投资人融资。这个市场里没有一家接受投资的公司成为持久的赢家。
We will not sell the company to a listed group. This damaged or ended 太傻 (under 华闻), 啄木鸟 (under 朴新), and 顺顺 (under 好未来).不把公司卖给上市集团。这分别损害或终结了太傻（被华闻收购）、啄木鸟（被朴新收购）和顺顺（被好未来收购）。
We will not run fake personal accounts or pay for reviews. Both practices get accounts banned and can be detected.不做伪装的个人账号，不购买评价。这两种做法都会导致账号被封，而且可以被识破。
We will not treat the US market as our main source of growth. It is a premium option only.不把美国市场当作主要增长引擎。它只是一个高端选项。
We will not publish success numbers we cannot prove. A claim like "99%" gives our next honest competitor a way to attack us.不发布无法证明的成功数字。像「99%」这样的宣称，会给下一个诚实的竞争对手留下攻击我们的把柄。

18Brand plan品牌规划

The founder leads at first; the company takes over later初期由创始人带头；后期交由机构承接

Stage 1 · Months 0 to 12 · Founder leads阶段一 · 0–12 月 · 创始人带头

The founder's own story and case work bring in clients — this is how Crimson started. This already runs today, as a paid and organic 小红书 operation across four accounts, with the founder's own account producing most of the qualified leads. Writing on 知乎 and building the public case log, starting with client 1, are the next two steps. At this stage, most new clients (over 70%) come through the founder directly. This is expected.创始人自身的故事和案例带来客户——Crimson 就是这样起步的。这项工作目前已经以「付费+自然流量」的形式，在四个小红书账号上运行，创始人自己的账号贡献了大部分优质线索。撰写知乎文章、从第 1 位客户起建立公开案例日志，是接下来的两步。这个阶段，大部分新客户（70% 以上）直接通过创始人获得，这是预期之内的情况。

Stage 2 · Months 12 to 24 · Build company-wide trust阶段二 · 12–24 月 · 建立机构层面的信任

We add named accounts for other consultants. Past clients write about their own applications. We publish a yearly bilingual report on admissions data — EIC's data reports show this earns years of free credibility. We publish reviews of each consultant, in public. All client contact records are stored in a company-owned 企业微信 account, so a consultant cannot leave with the client list.增设其他顾问的具名账号。老客户撰写自己的申请复盘。发布年度双语申请数据报告——启德的数据报告经验表明，这能换来多年的免费公信力。公开发布每位顾问的评价。所有客户联络记录存储于公司所有的企业微信账号中，顾问离职时无法带走客户名单。

Stage 3 · Months 24 to 36 · The product becomes the brand阶段三 · 24–36 月 · 产品即品牌

We build a school-matching tool, following the model Compass used to reach 1.16 million registered users. People come for the tool, not only for the founder. By this stage, we want less than 40% of new clients to come through the founder directly. This also reduces our risk if the founder is unavailable.开发一款选校匹配工具，参照指南者做到 116 万注册用户的模式。用户为工具而来，而不只是为创始人而来。到这个阶段，我们希望通过创始人直接获得的新客户占比降到 40% 以下。这也降低了创始人无法工作时的经营风险。

This gives us both things at once: the founder's face brings in clients now, and the company's systems keep the clients we have earned.这样我们能同时得到两样东西：创始人的形象现在负责带来客户，公司的系统负责留住我们已经赢得的客户。

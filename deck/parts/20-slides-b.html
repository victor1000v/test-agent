08Action 2 · Trust行动二 · 信任

Publish what competitors hide公开对手隐藏的信息

Every Chinese premium firm hides its prices. Hidden prices are linked to the most common complaint in this market. Western MBA advisors have published their per-school prices for 25 years, and this has not hurt their business. No firm in our price segment does this yet.每一家中国高端机构都不公开价格。价格不透明与这个市场最常见的投诉直接相关。西方 MBA 咨询机构按校公开定价已有 25 年历史，且并未因此影响生意。我们所在价格段目前没有一家机构这样做。

A public price page公开的价格页面

We publish the full price list and refund policy from day one. Menlo Coaching, a US firm, shows that a high published price attracts serious buyers and turns away price shoppers.从第一天起，公开完整价格表和退款政策。美国机构 Menlo Coaching 的经验表明：公开的高价格能吸引认真的买家，同时筛掉只比价的客户。

A yearly results report年度成果报告

Each year, from year one, we publish: number of clients, offer rate, and rate of getting into a client's first-choice school. We state our method. Only 2 of 141 firms in this market do this. A small, checked number is worth more than a large, unchecked claim.从第一年起，每年公开：客户数量、录取率、首选学校录取率，并说明统计方法。这个市场里 141 家公司中只有 2 家这样做。一个经过核实的小数字，比一个未经核实的大数字更有价值。

Public reviews, none removed公开评价，不做删除

Following the model used by 芝士圈, we let clients leave reviews for each consultant. We write down a policy that we will not delete negative reviews. One proven case of a deleted review would end client trust in a firm built on transparency.参照芝士圈的模式，允许客户对每位顾问发表评价。我们书面承诺不删除负面评价。一旦被坐实删除过评价，一家以透明为立身之本的公司就会失去客户信任。

Avoid避免

Do not write our own ranking articles, as 优越 does. Do not run paid advertising made to look like editorial content, as 新通 does. Do not publish success rates we cannot prove, such as "99%" or "zero complaints" — clients now check these claims and reject firms that make them.不要像优越那样自己撰写排名文章。不要像新通那样投放伪装成编辑内容的广告。不要发布无法证明的成功率，如「99%」或「零投诉」——客户现在会核实这些说法，并拒绝作出这类宣称的机构。

09Action 3 · Contract terms行动三 · 合同条款

Protect client money — and say so保护客户资金——并公开说明

After the 天道 case (¥14.23M unpaid) and the 太傻 and Valeon closures, losing a prepayment is the top fear among Chinese families. No premium Chinese firm currently answers this fear. We answer it with three contract rules:天道案例（欠款 1423 万元未付）以及太傻、再来人的倒闭之后，「预付款打水漂」已成为中国家庭的头号顾虑。目前没有一家中国高端机构回应这个顾虑。我们用三条合同规则来回应：

Payment is split into three stages: 30%, 40%, 30%付款分三阶段：30%、40%、30%

Client money in a separate account客户资金存入独立账户

Money for work not yet done stays in a separate, named account. We state this fact in our marketing. This is the direct answer to the 天道 case.未完成工作对应的款项，存放于独立且指定的账户。我们在营销中明确说明这一点。这是对天道案例的直接回应。

Fast refunds early on前期快速退款

We set clear points in the process where a client can leave and get a refund with no argument. We pay these refunds within days. The cost of these refunds counts as a marketing cost, not a loss.我们在流程中设定明确的节点，客户可在这些节点无条件退出并获得退款。退款在数日内到账。这类退款的成本视为营销支出，而非损失。

No guarantees, ever绝不提供任何担保

We do not offer 保录取 (guaranteed admission) or "full refund if you are not accepted." A guarantee is a debt on the company's books (see 太傻). It can also lead to fraud, as seen in AdmitWrite's court case. We may offer an optional bonus payment tied to a real result, such as an interview invitation.我们不提供保录取，也不提供「不录取全额退款」。担保本质上是公司账上的一笔负债（见太傻案例），也可能导向欺诈，如 AdmitWrite 案已被法院记录在案。我们可以设置一项可选的、与真实结果（如获得面试邀请）挂钩的奖励性付款。

Clients trust a named person more than a company logo. 启德's biggest weakness is that it has no founder to build trust around. Our plan uses two layers to avoid both problems:客户信任的是具名的个人，而不是公司标志。启德最大的弱点，正是没有创始人可以建立信任。但小工作室在唯一的社交账号被封时就会关门，而顺顺的案例证明，顾问离职时可能带走客户。我们的方案用两层结构来避免这两个问题：

Our account structure我们的账号结构

We post 4 to 7 times a week: results screenshots (November to March), case reviews, and analysis of industry problems. These formats get the most engagement. We reuse this content on 视频号.每周发帖 4–7 次：录取结果截图（11 月至 3 月）、案例复盘、行业问题分析。这些格式的互动率最高。这些内容会二次利用发布到视频号。

Cost to sign one client, by channel (RMB, estimated range)按渠道计算的单客户获取成本（人民币，估算区间）
The order of these channels matters more than the exact numbers. We will test real numbers with small ad budgets before we commit to a channel.这些渠道的相对排序，比具体数字更重要。在正式投入某个渠道前，我们会先用小额预算测试真实数据。

Our referral process我们的转介绍流程

Step 1: create a memorable moment on the day a client gets an offer. Step 2: ask for a referral at two points — when the client gets an offer, and when the client gets a visa. Step 3: pay ¥800 to ¥1,500 for each successful referral. Step 4: pay past clients to write a review of their application on 知乎. This builds a lasting record that new clients can search for and find.第一步：在客户拿到 offer 当天营造一个值得记住的仪式感时刻。第二步：在两个关键节点提出请求——客户拿到 offer 时，以及客户获得签证时。第三步：每次成功转介支付 800–1,500 元。第四步：付费邀请老客户在知乎撰写申请复盘。这会形成一份持久的、新客户可搜索到的口碑记录。

Targets目标

By year 2, we want 40% or more of new clients to come from referrals. The best firms in this market reach 40% to 70%. Referred clients sign at a rate of 50% to 80%. We want our average cost to sign one client under ¥2,500, against a ¥50,000 average sale — 5% of revenue, compared to 15% to 25% at large agencies.目标是到第二年，40% 或以上的新客户来自转介绍。这个市场里最优秀的机构能做到 40–70%。被转介的客户签约率为 50–80%。我们希望平均单客户获取成本控制在 2,500 元以内，对应 5 万元的平均客单价——占营收的 5%，而大型中介的这一比例为 15–25%。

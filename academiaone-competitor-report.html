The Masters Market Atlas 硕士申请市场全景图

Who is fighting for the Chinese postgraduate applicant — and how AcademiaOne wins. 谁在争夺中国研究生申请人——以及 AcademiaOne 如何胜出。

A field study of the companies that recruit, counsel, package and place Chinese students into Master's programmes worldwide — from Beijing's mega-agencies to London boutiques, from VC-fuelled platforms to the billion-dollar Crimson machine. Every claim is graded by confidence; every chapter ends in a decision. 这是一份关于「谁在服务中国硕士申请人」的实地研究——从北京的传统大所到伦敦的精品工作室，从烧钱的互联网平台到估值十亿美元的 Crimson。覆盖招生、咨询、文书、投递全链条。每一条数据都标注了可信度，每一章都以一个决策收尾。

Compiled from public web sources, company disclosures, funding databases, hiring platforms, complaint registries and community discussion, researched August 2026. Figures marked est. are directional estimates, not audited numbers. 数据来自公开网络信息、公司披露、融资数据库、招聘平台、投诉平台与社区讨论，检索时间为 2026 年 8 月。标注 est. 的为方向性估算，并非审计数字。

Chapter 02 · Part I第一部 · 第 02 章

Method & Data Confidence研究方法与数据可信度

How this atlas was built, what the numbers mean, and where to trust them — so the report can be used for real decisions, not just impressions. 这份报告如何构建、数字意味着什么、哪些可以信任——让它能支撑真实决策，而不只是留下印象。

This study was produced by parallel research teams, each assigned one competitive category, working simultaneously across English and Chinese sources: company websites and filings, Crunchbase / PitchBook / Tracxn / IT桔子-style funding databases, LinkedIn and Chinese hiring platforms (BOSS直聘, 职友集, 看准网), 小红书 / 知乎 / 抖音 / B站 account footprints, complaint registries (黑猫投诉, Trustpilot, Glassdoor, Reddit), industry white papers (启德, 新东方, IIE, HESA, UKCISA) and trade press. Four additional deep-dive teams covered the Crimson Education scaling case, Chinese outbound market data, customer-acquisition economics, and failure post-mortems. Two synthesis passes then extracted cross-company patterns and converted them into strategy. 本研究由多个并行研究小组完成，每组负责一个竞争类别，同时检索中英文信息源：公司官网与工商信息、Crunchbase / PitchBook / Tracxn / IT桔子等融资数据库、LinkedIn 与国内招聘平台（BOSS直聘、职友集、看准网）、小红书 / 知乎 / 抖音 / B站的账号矩阵、投诉平台（黑猫投诉、Trustpilot、Glassdoor、Reddit）、行业白皮书（启德、新东方、IIE、HESA、UKCISA）及行业媒体。另有四个专题小组分别深挖 Crimson 扩张案例、中国出国留学市场数据、获客经济学与失败案例复盘。最后经过两轮综合分析，从跨公司规律中提炼出战略建议。

The study covers companies across nine archetypes and four geographies. Traditional agencies, premium consultancies, online platforms, background-enhancement providers, communities, global recruiters, pathway operators, Western admissions firms and MBA specialists are all included — because AcademiaOne's client meets all of them on the way to a decision, and each archetype teaches a different lesson about what scales and what breaks. 研究覆盖九大机构类型、四大地域的公司。传统中介、高端咨询、互联网平台、背景提升机构、社区内容平台、国际招生集团、预科运营商、欧美申请公司与 MBA 专业咨询全部纳入——因为 AcademiaOne 的客户在做决策的路上会遇到所有这些玩家，而每种类型都揭示了「什么能规模化、什么会崩塌」的不同侧面。

How to read the confidence chips如何理解可信度标签

high高  Documented in filings, funding databases, audited accounts or multiple consistent public sources.有工商/上市披露、融资数据库、审计报表或多个一致的公开来源佐证。

medium中  Reported by credible press or the company itself, partially verifiable; ranges reflect the spread of sources.来自可信媒体或公司自述，可部分验证；区间反映了不同来源之间的差异。

low / est.低 / 估算  Directional estimates inferred from hiring volume, account footprints, pricing pages and analogous companies. Useful for shape, not for quotation.根据招聘规模、账号数据、价格页与同类公司推断的方向性估算。用于判断量级与趋势，不可直接引用为事实。

Three honest limits. First, private companies in this industry routinely inflate student counts and offer tallies; where marketing claims could not be corroborated, they are labelled as claims. Second, team sizes for Chinese firms are estimated from hiring platforms and office counts, which lag reality by months. Third, marketing-budget figures are modelled estimates — treat them as order-of-magnitude, and treat every strategic conclusion built on them as robust only where the pattern holds across many companies, which is exactly how Chapter 19 was constructed. 三个必须坦白的局限。第一，这个行业的私营公司普遍夸大学员数量与 offer 数据；凡无法交叉验证的宣传数字，报告中一律标注为「宣称」。第二，中国公司的团队规模由招聘平台与办公室数量推算，可能滞后于现实数月。第三，营销预算为模型估算——只能作为量级参考；因此本报告的战略结论只建立在「跨多家公司都成立的规律」之上，这正是第 19 章的构建方式。

Chapter 06 · Part III第三部 · 第 06 章

The Mega-Agencies传统大所

New Oriental, EIC, JJL, Aoji, Shinyway: the billion-RMB machines that industrialised study abroad. They are slower and more distrusted than ever — and still take the largest share of the market. Understanding why is the first lesson in this business. 新东方前途、启德、金吉列、澳际、新通：把留学做成流水线的十亿级机器。它们比以往任何时候都更迟缓、更不被信任——却仍占据最大的市场份额。理解「为什么」，是这门生意的第一课。

The traditional giants are still the market. New Oriental's study-abroad consulting arm alone booked an estimated US$700–850M in FY2024–25 — more than every boutique in this report combined — and EIC, JJL, Shinyway and New Channel each run branch networks with one to three thousand staff. Their machine is simple: brand age plus physical branches plus paid search buys the trust of parents, a commissioned sales floor converts it, and a production line of junior consultants delivers it. It works, and it is precisely why they are vulnerable: every part of the machine optimises for signing, not outcomes. 传统巨头依然「就是市场本身」。仅新东方前途出国的留学咨询业务，2024–25 财年估计就有 7–8.5 亿美元收入——超过本报告所有精品机构的总和；启德、金吉列、新通、新航道各自都运营着一千到三千人的分支网络。它们的机器很简单：品牌年头 + 线下门店 + 百度投放买来家长的信任，提成制销售团队负责转化，初级顾问流水线负责交付。这套机器有效，但也正是它的软肋所在：机器的每个环节都为「签单」而优化，而不是为「结果」。

The complaint record is structural, not accidental. Across 黑猫投诉 and forum archives the same patterns repeat by design: refund clauses that keep 50% after delivering only a school list (New Oriental), branch-manager promises that die with staff turnover (EIC), service that collapses the day after signing (JJL, Bailitop), and university-commission models that quietly steer students to partner schools (JJL's 2,600+ partners, IDP, Aoji). Meanwhile Tiandao — the closest thing the 2010s had to a scaled premium Masters brand — collapsed in 2022 with RMB 14M+ in unrefunded prepaid fees, and JiaHua ShiDa shows what happens when a moat built on regulatory licences is deregulated away. 巨头的投诉记录是结构性的，不是偶然的。翻遍黑猫投诉与论坛档案，同样的模式反复出现：只交付了一份选校清单就扣留 50% 费用的退款条款（新东方）、随经办人离职一起消失的口头承诺（启德）、签约次日服务即断崖式下滑（金吉列、百利天下）、以及悄悄把学生导向返佣院校的代理模式（金吉列 2600+ 合作院校、IDP、澳际）。与此同时，天道——2010 年代最接近「规模化高端硕士品牌」的公司——在 2022 年带着 1400 万+ 未退学费崩塌；嘉华世达则示范了「建立在牌照上的护城河，会随牌照制度一起被取消」。

Their digital transition is the open flank. Every giant is an institutional brand with no founder IP, running bland multi-account matrices on 小红书 while their most damaging complaint threads sit unanswered on the exact forums (一亩三分地, 知乎) where Masters applicants do their diligence. They still own lower-tier-city parents and walk-in traffic; they are visibly losing the RED-native generation that AcademiaOne serves. 数字化转型是它们敞开的侧翼。每家巨头都是没有创始人 IP 的机构品牌，在小红书上运营着面目模糊的矩阵账号，而最伤品牌的投诉长帖，就躺在硕士申请人做尽调的那些论坛上（一亩三分地、知乎）无人回应。它们仍然握有低线城市家长和到店客流，但正在肉眼可见地失去小红书原生的一代——恰恰是 AcademiaOne 服务的人群。

Copy:学习： EIC's annual White Paper (own a proprietary data report for UK/HK Masters); New Oriental's full-cycle LTV thinking (application → arrival → career); Shinyway's regional density (own one city's channels before expanding); Bailitop's proof that Masters clients can be served nationally with no branches.启德的年度《白皮书》（做一份自有的英港硕士申请数据报告）；新东方的全周期 LTV 思维（申请 → 落地 → 职业）；新通的区域纵深（先吃透一座城市的渠道再扩张）；百利天下证明了硕士客户完全可以无门店、全国化交付。

Exploit:利用： every giant's refund-trap contracts, consultant churn and commission steering are documented in public — AcademiaOne's counter-positioning (no university commissions, named senior consultants end-to-end, milestone billing, capped intake) writes itself from their complaint threads.巨头的退款陷阱条款、顾问流失与返佣导向都有公开记录——AcademiaOne 的反向定位（不拿院校返佣、指名资深顾问全程负责、按里程碑收费、限量接单）几乎可以直接从它们的投诉帖里抄出来。

Chapter 07 · Part III第三部 · 第 07 章

Premium Masters Boutiques高端硕博申请机构

GGU, PalmDrive, Compass, Valeon and their cohort are AcademiaOne’s true reference class: premium prices, mentor networks, outcome-led brands. This chapter reads their playbooks line by line — pricing, people, promises, and cracks. 世毕盟、棕榈大道、指南者、再来人及其同类，才是 AcademiaOne 真正的参照系：高客单价、导师网络、以结果立品牌。本章逐行拆解它们的打法——定价、团队、承诺，以及裂缝。

This category split into two poles, and in December 2025 one of them collapsed. Valeon (再来人) — the decade's benchmark for the "elite part-time mentor + full-time supervisor" premium model, charging RMB 120k average for postgraduate packages — ceased operations after a year of wage arrears. Its post-mortem is arithmetic: mentor fees of RMB 30–50k per student, total delivery cost RMB 60–70k, gross margin under 30%, three tier-1-city flagship offices, refund liabilities from admission guarantees, and Heimao complaints rising from 27 (2022) to 186 (2025). The same structural weakness — part-time celebrity mentors who go quiet in peak season — is the number-one complaint against GGU (世毕盟), whose Beijing entity shows only ~15 insured staff behind RMB 80–100k packages, and against PalmDrive (棕榈大道)'s 4,000-mentor pool. 这个类别分裂成两极，而其中一极在 2025 年 12 月崩塌了。再来人（Valeon）——十年来「名校兼职导师 + 全职学术督导」高端模式的标杆，研究生套餐均价 12 万元——在拖欠工资一年后彻底停业。它的尸检报告就是一道算术题：每个学生 3–5 万元的导师成本、6–7 万元的总交付成本、不到 30% 的毛利、三个一线城市的旗舰办公室、保录承诺带来的退款负债，以及从 27 件（2022）涨到 186 件（2025）的黑猫投诉。同样的结构性弱点——旺季消失的兼职名校导师——也是世毕盟（GGU）被投诉最多的一条（8–10 万的套餐背后，北京主体参保员工仅约 15 人），也是棕榈大道那个 4000 人导师池的死穴。

The other pole is quietly winning. Compass (指南者留学) — bootstrapped, all-full-time, "3-to-1" delivery, published prices of RMB 18–24k, twelve written service guarantees, and a program-database app with 1.16M registered users that manufactures its own demand — served 2,656 clients in the 2025 season. It proves three things at once: transparency converts, full-time delivery kills the mentor-flakiness complaint class, and a data product is the cheapest durable acquisition channel in this industry. Its ceiling is equally instructive: at RMB 20k it cannot afford same-field PhD mentors or serious US/PhD capability — the depth stays unserved. 另一极正在悄悄取胜。指南者留学——零融资、全职交付、「三对一」、公开定价 1.8–2.4 万、十二项书面服务承诺、外加一个 116 万注册用户的选校数据库 App 自造流量——2025 申请季服务了 2,656 名客户。它同时证明了三件事：透明本身就能转化；全职交付彻底消灭了「导师失联」这一整类投诉；数据产品是这个行业最便宜、最持久的获客渠道。它的天花板同样有启发性：2 万元的客单价养不起同领域 PhD 导师，也撑不起像样的美国/博士申请能力——「深度」这块市场仍然空着。

Between the poles sit the instructive middles: 毕达 (BetterEdu)'s meet-your-consultant-before-signing and named public client endorsements; BeBeyond's 25-year-old workshop model (highest integrity, zero funnel); Stoooges (三士渡) proving that paying top-of-market for full-time elite talent plus a genuinely funny content voice is a moat — and that payroll can eat the margin; Topsedu's "Top-30 or refund" guarantees showing how outcome promises become balance-sheet bombs. The premium slot — Compass-grade transparency with GGU-grade depth, minus the part-time mentor lottery — is structurally vacant. That is AcademiaOne's slot. 两极之间是几家极具启发性的中间样本：毕达教育的「签约前先见顾问」与实名公开的客户代言；BeBeyond 二十五年的工作坊模式（操守最高、漏斗为零）；三士渡证明了「顶薪雇全职名校人才 + 真正有趣的内容语气」是一条护城河——也证明了工资单能吃掉利润；托普仕的「不进 Top30 就退款」示范了结果承诺如何变成资产负债表上的炸弹。而「指南者级的透明 + 世毕盟级的深度 − 兼职导师抽奖」这个高端席位，在结构上是空着的。那就是 AcademiaOne 的位置。

The vacant slot, precisely specified那个空着的席位，精确定义

Compass-grade trust mechanics (published prices, written SLAs, meet-your-consultant-first, verifiable outcomes) × GGU-grade depth (genuine same-field academic mentorship) × delivered by accountable full-time or properly contracted senior staff with caseload caps — at RMB 40–80k, between Compass's 20k commodity tier and Valeon's fatal 120k. The Platform Graveyard平台模式的沉浮

Between 2014 and 2019, venture capital poured into “free” study-abroad platforms that would disrupt the agencies. Almost every one of them stalled, shrank or died. Their post-mortems are the cheapest tuition AcademiaOne will ever pay. 2014–2019 年，风险资本涌入号称要颠覆传统中介的「免费留学平台」。它们几乎全部停滞、萎缩或死亡。这些尸检报告，是 AcademiaOne 能上到的最便宜的一课。

Between 2013 and 2018, venture capital funded a single thesis over and over: applications should be free — universities pay commissions, an app matches students to schools, and the agency middleman dies. 51offer raised US$50M from NEA and Starr on that thesis; myOffer/学无国界 raised an estimated RMB 100–200M; ShunShun took US$18M from TAL and was absorbed within a year; ApplySquare raised ~US$10M to disintermediate agencies with data; 柳橙网 became the sector's first NEEQ listing as a "Taobao for study abroad". By 2026 every one of them is diminished, pivoted, absorbed or zombie. Nobody was killed by a competitor. They were killed by the product. 2013 到 2018 年，风险资本把同一个论点反复投了一遍：申请应该免费——大学付佣金，App 做匹配，中介这个中间人去死。51offer 靠这个论点从 NEA 和史带融了 5000 万美元；学无国界 myOffer 累计融资约 1–2 亿元人民币；顺顺留学拿了好未来 1800 万美元、一年内被收编；申请方融了约千万美元要用数据消灭中介；柳橙网成了行业第一家新三板挂牌的「留学淘宝」。到 2026 年，它们全部萎缩、转型、被吞并或僵尸化。没有一家是被对手杀死的。杀死它们的是产品本身。

The arithmetic never worked. Admissions is a low-frequency, high-stakes trust purchase: CAC stays high because nobody buys twice, commissions are capped per student, and "free" structurally means steering students toward whichever university pays — a conflict applicants eventually smelled. Winning the home turf如何赢下主场

(1) Productise real UK-ground delivery — campus visits, UK academics on strategy calls, in-UK onboarding — the thing every rival claims and none sells at depth. (2) Make commission-free independence explicit and loud: "we are paid only by you" is the direct counter to UKEC/金矢/SUUK's structure. (3) Build the in-UK interception channel Downton proved: Chinese undergraduates already at UK universities applying onward to Masters/PhD are reachable at near-zero CAC through campus presence. (4) Speak to the residency motive in the HK/SG line without becoming an immigration shop. (5) Own your name's search results completely — the micro-boutiques in this table show what happens when a referred parent googles you and finds nothing.（1）把真实的英国本地交付产品化——校园探访、英国学者参与选校策略、在英落地服务——这是每个对手都宣称、没有一家深度出售的东西。（2）把「不拿佣金」的独立性放大声讲：「我们只收你的钱」是对 UKEC/金矢/SUUK 结构的正面反制。（3）建设唐顿验证过的在英拦截渠道：已在英国读本科、要继续申硕博的中国学生，通过校园存在几乎零成本触达。（4）在港新线上直面「身份动机」，但不变成移民中介。（5）彻底占领自己品牌名的搜索结果——本表里那些微型机构证明了：当被转介的家长搜索你却什么都找不到时，会发生什么。

# 获客篇: Customer Acquisition Economics for China Study-Abroad Services, 2025-26

# Customer Acquisition for 留学服务 in China, 2025-2026 (获客篇)

**Methodology note (read first):** live web search was unavailable in this research session (the workflow's search budget was exhausted before this deep-dive began), so every figure below is an analyst estimate synthesized from industry data, platform advertiser benchmarks, agency-operator disclosures and vertical media reporting available through early 2026 — not freshly scraped August-2026 numbers. Ranges are deliberately wide. The correct way to use this document is as a prior: validate the paid-channel numbers with RMB 3,000–10,000 test budgets on 聚光/知+ before committing, and validate funnel rates against AcademiaOne's own CRM within one intake cycle. ## 1. The structural picture: why acquisition economics decide who survives

China's outbound study market has recovered to roughly 700,000–850,000 students going abroad per year, with the UK the single largest destination for one-year Master's degrees and Hong Kong/Singapore the fastest-growing substitutes. The serviceable market for application consulting (中介/咨询服务, excluding language training) is plausibly RMB 30–50 billion. Three structural facts shape acquisition:

1. **The decision journey moved to 小红书.** For the core Master's applicant (a 20–24-year-old undergraduate, female-skewing, researching for herself rather than through her parents), 小红书 has replaced Baidu as the first and heaviest research surface. Baidu SEM still captures parent-driven and undergraduate/低龄 demand, but for the UK/HK/SG Master's segment AcademiaOne targets, the funnel begins with XHS search ("英国一年硕", "港三新二", "留学中介避雷") months before any consultation.
2. **Lead costs have inflated across every paid channel** as thousands of 工作室 (micro-studios, often founded by ex-顾问 of big agencies) crowd into the same content formats. The result is an arms race in 素人矩阵 (fake-organic account farms) and a corresponding platform crackdown that makes paid-organic hybrids operationally risky.
3. **Trust collapsed faster than prices.** Years of 保录取 scandals, 退费难 complaints and mid-size agency 跑路 events (2023–2025) mean the highest-converting asset in the category is now *verifiable* proof — named cases, contract transparency, refund clarity — not reach. This is the single biggest tailwind for a boutique with a real UK footprint.

## 2. Channel-by-channel economics

### 2.1 小红书 (the primary battlefield)

**机构号运营 (official professional account).** Verification costs ~RMB 600/year and unlocks 私信通 (compliant in-app DM), 表单 components and 聚光 advertising. A serious operation posts 4–7 notes/week: offer 晒单 (offer screenshots, peak season November–March), 选校对比 carousels, 案例复盘, 避雷/行业黑幕 content (highest engagement format in the category), and application-timeline checklists. Organic reach for a well-run 机构号 is feast-or-famine: baseline notes get 200–2,000 views; a 爆款 (usually 避雷 or 案例逆袭 framing) can pull 50,000–500,000 and generate 100–1,000 private messages in a week. Fully-loaded content cost (one competent operator + design) runs RMB 10,000–20,000/month for a studio, RMB 50,000+ for a multi-account 大机构 matrix.

**聚光平台 (paid ads).** 留学 sits in the education vertical and requires business-license qualification. Estimated benchmarks: feed CPC RMB 1–5, search CPC on high-intent 留学 keywords RMB 5–15; 私信开口成本 (cost per initiated DM conversation) RMB 30–150; 表单/留资成本 (cost per lead form) RMB 100–400, with tier-1-city, high-ticket targeting at the top of the range. Search ads on your own brand terms and competitor-adjacent terms ("XX留学怎么样") are the highest-ROI placement and cheap to defend.

**报备笔记 / 蒲公英 (KOL cooperation).** Commercial notes must be declared through 蒲公英, which takes a ~10% platform commission on influencer deals. Mid-tier 留学博主 (10k–100k followers) charge roughly RMB 1,000–10,000 per 报备笔记. Declared notes are throttled less but convert visibly worse than "organic-looking" content — which is exactly what drives the gray-market alternative below.

**薯条 (content boost).** Roughly RMB 100 buys on the order of 4,000–10,000 impressions on a compliant, non-salesy note. The standard SOP is: publish 10–20 notes, 薯条-test each with RMB 100–300, then pour budget (or 聚光) behind the one that shows organic escape velocity.

**素人矩阵 (undeclared KOC farms) and 违规/封号 risk.** The dominant gray tactic 2023–2025: agencies run 10–50 "ordinary student" accounts posting scripted 经验帖 that funnel DMs to sales 微信. Ghost-written 素人笔记 trade at RMB 30–300 per post; 代运营 firms sell full matrix operation at RMB 5,000–30,000/month. 留学 is one of XHS's most-policed categories (alongside medical aesthetics and education), and enforcement waves against 虚假营销 hit matrix accounts hard: detection of coordinated posting, 导流 keywords (微信号 in images, homophone spellings), or guarantee language triggers 限流 → 禁言 → 封号. Practical consequence: a matrix can be zeroed overnight, and the 机构号 itself can be collaterally banned if linked. For a durable brand, the correct posture is one flagship 机构号 + 2–4 real-human consultant/founder sub-IP accounts + declared KOL buys — no undeclared farms.

### 2.2 知乎 (the compounding moat)

知乎's value in 留学 is long-tail search: high-quality answers to evaluative queries ("XX大学commerce硕士值得读吗", "留学中介怎么选") rank in both 知乎 internal search and Baidu SEO for years. Content half-life is measured in years versus days on XHS. Paid amplification via 知+ runs an estimated CPC of RMB 2–5 with conversion plugins to landing pages/微信. Audience skews male, STEM, rational, higher-intent-but-slower — it is the natural home for 世毕盟-style rigorous long-form. The classic SOP (pioneered at scale by 世毕盟) is student-authored 申请总结 posts: alumni write detailed application retrospectives naming the agency, often incentivized with rebates or gifts, seeding hundreds of quasi-testimonials into search. Lead volume per month is small (a good 知乎 presence might yield 10–50 consultations/month), but blended CAC amortized over content lifetime is among the lowest of any channel, and the SEO residue defends brand-name searches.

### 2.3 抖音 / 视频号 (volume machines for 大机构)

The playbook is 直播 + low-ticket front-end: livestream rooms sell RMB 9.9–199 "留学规划课/测评" whose real product is a phone number for the 电销 team, with 巨量千川 spend behind it. Estimated education-vertical CPL is RMB 60–200, but lead quality is materially lower (curiosity clicks, parents of younger children, price shoppers), so 咨询→签约 on Douyin-origin leads runs low single digits to ~10%, and true signed-client CAC lands around RMB 3,000–8,000 — viable only with a professional telesales floor, i.e., 大机构 economics (金吉列, 新东方前途, 启德 all operate here). 视频号 is the rising parent-side channel (45–55 demographic, native to the WeChat ecosystem, frictionless handoff to 企业微信) and is worth a light presence for any firm whose fees are parent-paid. For a boutique, Douyin livestreaming is a distraction; repurposing XHS content into 视频号 clips is the cheap, sensible version.

### 2.4 B站

Niche but trust-dense: 院校解析 long videos, 留学vlog collaborations, alumni interviews. Direct lead volume is low; its role is mid-funnel credibility for applicants doing deep diligence. Sensible allocation: near-zero, or repost long-form content produced for other purposes.

### 2.5 公众号 + 私域/企业微信 (the conversion engine, not an acquisition channel)

公众号 open rates have decayed to ~1–5%, but the account remains the trust anchor (functions as the "official website" in Chinese diligence behavior, plus WeChat 搜一搜 SEO). The money is in the 企业微信 SOP that every serious player now runs: 渠道活码 (channel-tagged QR) → automated welcome + lead magnet (选校手册, 案例集, 定位测评) → tagging by grade/major/target region → drip SOP (day-1 value content, day-3 case study, day-7 webinar invite) → consultant 朋友圈 persona operation (3–5 posts/week: offers, work-in-progress, student life) → free 1v1 定位评估 → written proposal → contract. Typical cycle from add-to-WeChat to signature is 2 weeks–6 months (peak signing: spring of the applicant's penultimate year through early final year). 企业微信 rather than personal 微信 matters strategically: it keeps the client asset with the company when consultants leave — the single most common way studios lose their book of business.

### 2.6 百度SEM (expensive legacy, parent-facing)

留学 keywords are historically among the most expensive in Chinese SEM: "留学中介"-class head terms have long traded at RMB 100–300+ per click, with peaks higher during application season. Estimated valid-lead cost is RMB 300–1,000, and signed-client CAC through SEM commonly reaches RMB 5,000–15,000. It still works for 大机构 selling to parents (and for 低龄/本科 segments) and for defending brand terms cheaply, but for a boutique targeting self-directed Master's applicants it is the worst channel per yuan. The minimum viable Baidu play is free: 百家号/知道/贴吧 SEO residue plus a defensive brand-term budget of a few hundred RMB/month.

### 2.7 淘宝 / 闲鱼 / 小程序 storefronts

淘宝 and 闲鱼 host a large price-shopping market: 文书单项 at RMB 800–3,000, semi-DIY packages at RMB 5,000–15,000, plus a gray fringe of 学长学姐 freelancers on 闲鱼. Volume exists but the traffic is structurally price-first, refund-prone, and brand-corrosive for a premium positioning; upsell rates from single-item to full-service are modest. 小程序 is different in kind: a 选校定位工具/案例库 mini-program is a lead magnet, not a storefront — 指南者 proved this model (see 3.1). Recommended posture for AcademiaOne: no 淘宝/闲鱼 presence; consider a lightweight case-database or school-matcher tool later.

### 2.8 校园代理 / 线下讲座

Campus ambassadors (学生大使) are paid roughly RMB 500–3,000 per signed referral or 5–10% commission; they distribute in class groups, run 打卡 communities and staff campus talks. 讲座进校 works through faculty/协会 relationships and is especially strong at 中外合作办学 institutions (2+2/3+1 programs), where institutional cooperation deals can carry 10–25% referral economics but require B2B relationship investment. This channel is geography-bound — hard for a UK-headquartered firm without mainland staff, easy to add later via part-time campus reps at 5–10 target universities (the 双非-strong, HK/UK-bound schools where AcademiaOne's case studies resonate).

### 2.9 转介绍 (referral — the best economics in the category)

Top boutiques report 40–70% of new signings from referral and alumni networks. Standard mechanics: 老带新 rewards of RMB 500–2,000 (cash/gift card) or 5–10% of contract value; referred leads sign at 50–80% because trust is pre-transferred. Effective CAC: RMB 500–2,000, an order of magnitude below any paid channel. The operational insight is that referral is not passive — the SOP is: engineered "wow moments" (offer-day rituals, handwritten notes), a formal ask at the two peak-gratitude moments (offer received; visa granted), alumni community maintenance, and student-authored 申请总结 (the 世毕盟 mechanism) that converts gratitude into permanent search-visible content.

## 3. CAC and funnel benchmarks by company type

**大机构 (multi-city agencies, 客单价 RMB 30k–60k):** paid-heavy mix (SEM, Douyin, XHS 聚光) feeding telesales. Marketing + sales commonly consumes 15–25% of revenue; blended signed-client CAC estimated at RMB 3,000–8,000, with SEM/Douyin cohorts worse. Telesales 咨询→签约 runs 5–15%.

**工作室 (studios, 客单价 RMB 15k–40k; premium boutiques RMB 60k–150k):** organic-content + referral mix; blended CAC RMB 500–2,500. Because leads arrive pre-sold by content, 咨询→签约 reaches 25–50%.

**Representative full-funnel (XHS-origin, studio):** note view → DM 0.5–3% on converting notes; DM → WeChat add 30–60%; WeChat → substantive consultation 30–50%; consultation → signature 25–50%. Net: roughly 1 signing per 5,000–30,000 targeted impressions, heavily dependent on content quality and season.

## 4. Content SOPs of the benchmark players

- **指南者留学:** the product-led acquisition benchmark. Its moat is a structured 案例库 + 选校定位工具 (App/小程序) with tens of thousands of admitted-case records; users self-serve "can I get in?" queries and surrender contact data for full access. Around it sits a standardized 公众号/XHS matrix segmented by region and 院校, a semi-DIY lower-price product line to monetize price-sensitive leads, and relentless offer-season 晒单 cadence. Lesson: turn your case history into a queryable product, not just posts.
- **棕榈大道:** the IP-matrix benchmark. Grew on elite-alumni personal brands and long-form 公众号 storytelling, funneled through low-price 训练营/体验营 into high-ticket consulting, then progressively institutionalized the IPs under a company brand. Lesson: personal stories acquire; the brand must own the funnel.
- **世毕盟 (GGU):** the referral/word-of-mouth benchmark. Minimal paid marketing; penetration of 清北/华五 campuses via mentor networks, plus the flood of student-written 申请总结 on 知乎 that functions as permanent SEO testimonial infrastructure. Highest 客单价 tolerance in the sample. Lesson: at the premium end, proof and peer networks beat media spend entirely.

## 5. Influencer-guru vs. institutional brand — and why the head players are 去个人化

The guru model (founder-IP dominates all channels) has the lowest CAC and highest consult-to-sign conversion in the industry — trust in a person converts better than trust in a logo. But the top firms are deliberately de-personalizing because the model carries four structural liabilities: (1) key-person risk — one platform ban, scandal or burnout zeroes the funnel; (2) asset leakage — consultant-IPs who leave take clients with them (the industry's most common studio-death cause, and why 企业微信 + client-ownership clauses became standard); (3) a scaling ceiling — one person cannot serve growth, and delegation breaks the promise the IP made; (4) valuation — buyers and investors discount founder-bound revenue. The durable synthesis, and the explicit recommendation for AcademiaOne: an institutional brand as the container of proof (cases, contracts, guarantees of process not outcome), with a *managed matrix* of real-named consultant IPs — including the founder — whose private-domain assets live in 企业微信 owned by the company. Personal enough to convert, institutional enough to survive.

## 6. Compliance red lines (广告法 and platform rules)

- **保offer marketing is illegal, not merely risky.** 广告法 Article 24 prohibits education/training advertising from making express *or implied* guarantees of admission, degrees or exam success. "保录取", "100%成功", "不录取全退" phrasing (and case imagery implying it) has produced administrative fines typically in the RMB 100k–1M band under Article 58. Structuring refunds contractually while never advertising outcomes is the compliant pattern.
- **保录取 fraud is now criminal territory.** 2023–2025 exposés of forged transcripts and QS-top-50 "guaranteed placement" schemes, plus Hong Kong universities' fraud prosecutions and tightened verification, mean any association with 保录 language now damages conversion with the informed segment AcademiaOne wants.
- **Platform rules:** XHS requires qualification for education ads, polices guarantee language and off-platform 导流 in organic content, and mass-bans 素人矩阵 in enforcement waves; undeclared commercial notes violate both platform rules and 广告法 disclosure norms.
- **Consumer-protection backdrop:** 退费难 and agency 跑路 cases pushed regulators toward model-contract guidance; advertising transparent refund terms and contract samples is both compliant and a conversion weapon.

## 7. Recommended acquisition architecture for AcademiaOne

Year-one mix (indicative, assuming RMB 15k–30k/month total budget): 50% content engine — one XHS 机构号 + founder IP account + 知乎 long-form program (2 pillar answers/month) with everything repurposed to 视频号/B站; 20% 聚光 search + brand-defense (validate at RMB 100–400/lead before scaling); 0% Baidu SEM and Douyin livestreaming; 10% declared 蒲公英 micro-KOL tests; 20% referral program (formal 老带新 rewards, offer-day rituals, incentivized student 申请总结). Target steady state within 18 months: 50%+ of signings from referral + organic search residue, blended CAC under RMB 2,000 against a RMB 30k+ 客单价, and a compliance-clean "no guarantees, only verifiable cases" brand line that converts precisely the burned-by-中介 segment the scandals created. Every number above should be re-based against live 聚光/知+ test data and AcademiaOne's own funnel within the first intake cycle.

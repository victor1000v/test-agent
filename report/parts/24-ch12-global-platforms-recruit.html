Global Platforms & Recruiters国际平台与招生巨头

IDP, ApplyBoard, Leverage and the commission-funded recruiting layer: enormous volume, thin margins, and total exposure to visa politics. What the 2024–26 policy shocks did to them is a warning about business-model dependency. IDP、ApplyBoard、Leverage 与整个佣金驱动的招生层：体量巨大、利润微薄、完全暴露在签证政治之下。2024–26 年政策冲击对它们的打击，是关于商业模式依赖性的一记警钟。

2024–2026 ran a controlled experiment on the commission-recruitment model, and the results are in. IDP Education — the industry's blue chip, co-owner of IELTS, ASX-listed — guided placement volumes down ~29% in June 2025 and lost ~48% of its market value in one session; from its 2021 peak of A$10B+ it now trades around A$1B. ApplyBoard, the C$4B unicorn built on the India→Canada corridor, has been written down more than 80% by Fidelity after Canada's permit caps halved its core flow; its founder-CEO left mid-crisis. Adventus.io, the marketplace that was supposed to be the safe "picks and shovels" play, entered administration in late 2024 — holding commissions it owed to its own sub-agents. Leverage Edu weathered layoffs and toxic-culture allegations while pivoting its story toward an IPO. Three Anglosphere governments tightened visas at once, and what looked like four-market diversification was revealed as one correlated trade. 2024–2026 年，市场为佣金招生模式做了一场对照实验，结果已经揭晓。IDP——行业蓝筹、雅思共有方、澳交所上市公司——2025 年 6 月下调全年安置量指引约 29%，单个交易日市值蒸发约 48%；从 2021 年 100 多亿澳元的峰值跌到如今 10 亿澳元左右。押注「印度→加拿大」通道的 40 亿加元独角兽 ApplyBoard，在加拿大学签配额腰斩其核心流量后，被富达减记超过 80%，创始 CEO 中途离场。号称最安全的「卖铲子」平台 Adventus.io 于 2024 年底进入破产管理——倒下时手里还攥着欠自己分销代理的佣金。Leverage Edu 一边裁员、一边应付「毒性文化」指控，一边把故事包装成 IPO 叙事。三个英语国家政府同时收紧签证，所谓的「四市场多元化」被证明只是同一笔高度相关的交易。

Two survivors show what does work. India's Leap group (US$218M raised, incl. Yocket) anchors its economics in student loans rather than commissions — counselling is a customer-acquisition cost for fintech margins, which is why a boutique should never try to match its CAC. And SI-UK, AcademiaOne's most instructive direct rival, proves something important: with 72+ offices and a "free, official British brand" wrapper it still successfully sells paid premium tiers in China (est. RMB 10–30k) — evidence that Chinese families will pay on top of a "free" industry when the brand feels authoritative and British. Its weaknesses are equally instructive: a franchise quality lottery, commission-plus-fees double-dipping, and thin review real estate. ApplyBoard's deepest lesson is the political one: low-quality volume recruitment created the backlash that destroyed its own market. A boutique that sends genuinely qualified students to genuinely suitable programmes is on the right side of every future crackdown. 两个幸存者展示了什么才行得通。印度的 Leap 集团（融资 2.18 亿美元，旗下含 Yocket）把经济模型锚定在留学贷款而非佣金上——咨询只是金融业务的获客成本，这正是精品机构永远不该去对标其获客成本的原因。而 SI-UK，AcademiaOne 最值得研究的直接对手，证明了一件重要的事：即便顶着 72+ 办公室、「免费、官方、英国品牌」的外壳，它在中国依然成功卖出付费高端套餐（估计 1–3 万元）——说明只要品牌显得权威且「英国」，中国家庭愿意在一个「免费」行业之上继续掏钱。它的弱点同样有启发性：加盟制的服务质量抽奖、「佣金+服务费」两头收钱、单薄的评价资产。ApplyBoard 最深的教训则是政治性的：低质量的走量招生，亲手制造了摧毁自己市场的政策反弹。把真正合格的学生送进真正合适的项目的精品机构，站在未来每一次收紧的正确一边。

Background & Adjacent Services背景提升与周边服务

Research programmes, publication mills, internship placement, coursework tutoring: the services bolted onto the application itself. A revenue expansion map — and an ethical minefield with real regulatory tail-risk. 科研项目、论文产出、实习内推、课业辅导：围绕申请本身长出的服务群。这既是一张收入扩张地图，也是一片有真实监管尾部风险的道德雷区。

Around the application itself has grown a second economy — research programmes, competition coaching, internship placement, coursework tutoring, dissertation "guidance" — and it runs on a spectrum from legitimate to criminal. At the white end, ViaX盐趣 (Matrix-backed, ~US$15M raised) and 集思未来 GEC (New Oriental-invested, est. 500–1,000 staff) sell supervised research with real professors at RMB 5–50k; 翰林 Linstitute owns the competition-credential niche; 易美 monetises ex-admissions-officer review and Fortune-500 internships for HNW families at a reported US$80–100 CAC. In the middle, the industrial tutoring machines — 海马课堂 (claimed 10,000 tutors), 万能班长 (~540 staff, 3,000 tutors), 考而思, 辅无忧 — sell "GPA management" and 挂科申诉 at RMB 200–600/hour, shading into territory UK and Australian law now defines as contract cheating. At the black end, essay mills like HotEssay and Meeloun operate openly toward Chinese customers despite the UK's 2022 criminalisation of providing such services and China's 2025 Degree Law, which mandates degree revocation for ghostwritten theses. 申请这件事的周围，长出了第二个经济体——科研项目、竞赛辅导、实习内推、课业辅导、论文「指导」——它运行在一条从合法到犯罪的光谱上。白色一端，ViaX盐趣（经纬投资，累计融资约 1500 万美元）和集思未来（新东方入股，估计 500–1000 名员工）以 0.5–5 万元出售真实教授带队的科研督导；翰林占据竞赛履历生态位；易美面向高净值家庭出售前招生官审阅与名企实习，获客成本据称仅 80–100 美元。中间地带，工业化课业辅导机器——海马课堂（宣称万名导师）、万能班长（约 540 员工、3000 导师）、考而思、辅无忧——以每小时 200–600 元出售「GPA 管理」与挂科申诉，正滑向英澳法律定义的「代考代写协助（contract cheating）」灰区。黑色一端，HotEssay、Meeloun 这类论文工厂公然面向中国客户经营——尽管英国 2022 年已将提供此类服务入刑，中国 2025 年施行的《学位法》也明确对代写论文者撤销学位。

Two things are simultaneously true. First, the demand is real and adjacent to AcademiaOne's client: proven willingness-to-pay of RMB 5–50k for genuine academic development, and a desperate, unmet need for legitimate dissertation-stage skills coaching that the mills currently monetise. Second, the category is the industry's next reputational landmine: Xinhua's 2022 exposé of 背景提升乱象, the 2024–25 MOE rectification campaigns, and admissions committees' growing discount of purchased research all point one way. The line between "we coach your writing" and "we write it" is now a legal line in AcademiaOne's home jurisdiction. 两件事同时为真。第一，需求是真实的，而且就长在 AcademiaOne 的客户身上：0.5–5 万元买真实学术成长的付费意愿已被验证；论文阶段的合规学术技能辅导存在巨大的未满足需求——目前被论文工厂在黑市里变现。第二，这个品类是行业的下一颗声誉地雷：新华社 2022 年对背景提升乱象的曝光、教育部 2024–25 年的专项整治、以及招生委员会对「购买来的科研」越来越重的折价，都指向同一个方向。「我们辅导你写」与「我们替你写」之间的那条线，在 AcademiaOne 的注册地已经是一条法律红线。

White — build on it白 · 可以经营

Supervised research with named academics (ViaX, GEC), competition coaching (翰林), skills tutoring, pre-departure bridging, appeals guidance. Sold honestly, this is legitimate LTV expansion.具名学者督导的科研（ViaX、集思）、竞赛辅导（翰林）、学术技能辅导、行前衔接、申诉指导。诚实出售，这是合法的 LTV 扩张。

Grey — radioactive灰 · 有放射性

The tutoring giants earn their volume in the middle band — which is exactly why a UK-regulated consultancy cannot follow them there.辅导巨头的营收体量都在中间那条带上——这正是一家受英国法律约束的咨询公司不能跟进去的原因。

AcademiaOne's rules of engagementAcademiaOne 的交战守则

(1) Publish a visible academic-integrity charter and train every staff member on where guidance legally ends — it is simultaneously compliance and marketing in a market poisoned by the black end. (2) Capture the mills' demand legitimately: a structured academic-writing and dissertation-skills coaching product, with explicit boundaries, sold to your own admitted clients. (3) Partner with — never build — research providers, and only those with named academics and no publication guarantees. (4) Own the lifecycle the tutoring giants proved exists: pre-departure bridging and first-semester support are natural, clean, high-margin extensions for admitted clients.（1）公开一份学术操守章程，并训练每位员工弄清「指导」的法律边界在哪里——在一个被黑色端污染的市场里，这既是合规也是营销。（2）合法地承接论文工厂证明了的需求：面向自己的已录取客户，出售有明确边界的学术写作与论文技能辅导产品。（3）科研只做合作、不做自营——且只与有具名学者、不承诺发表的机构合作。（4）占据辅导巨头验证过的生命周期：行前衔接与第一学期支持，是干净、高毛利、顺理成章的延伸产品。

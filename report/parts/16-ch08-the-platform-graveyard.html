The Platform Graveyard平台模式的沉浮

Between 2014 and 2019, venture capital poured into “free” study-abroad platforms that would disrupt the agencies. Almost every one of them stalled, shrank or died. Their post-mortems are the cheapest tuition AcademiaOne will ever pay. 2014–2019 年，风险资本涌入号称要颠覆传统中介的「免费留学平台」。它们几乎全部停滞、萎缩或死亡。这些尸检报告，是 AcademiaOne 能上到的最便宜的一课。

Between 2013 and 2018, venture capital funded a single thesis over and over: applications should be free — universities pay commissions, an app matches students to schools, and the agency middleman dies. 51offer raised US$50M from NEA and Starr on that thesis; myOffer/学无国界 raised an estimated RMB 100–200M; ShunShun took US$18M from TAL and was absorbed within a year; ApplySquare raised ~US$10M to disintermediate agencies with data; 柳橙网 became the sector's first NEEQ listing as a "Taobao for study abroad". By 2026 every one of them is diminished, pivoted, absorbed or zombie. Nobody was killed by a competitor. They were killed by the product. 2013 到 2018 年，风险资本把同一个论点反复投了一遍：申请应该免费——大学付佣金，App 做匹配，中介这个中间人去死。51offer 靠这个论点从 NEA 和史带融了 5000 万美元；学无国界 myOffer 累计融资约 1–2 亿元人民币；顺顺留学拿了好未来 1800 万美元、一年内被收编；申请方融了约千万美元要用数据消灭中介；柳橙网成了行业第一家新三板挂牌的「留学淘宝」。到 2026 年，它们全部萎缩、转型、被吞并或僵尸化。没有一家是被对手杀死的。杀死它们的是产品本身。

The arithmetic never worked. Admissions is a low-frequency, high-stakes trust purchase: CAC stays high because nobody buys twice, commissions are capped per student, and "free" structurally means steering students toward whichever university pays — a conflict applicants eventually smelled. The exceptions prove the rule: 芥末留学 survives profitably because it owns one corridor (Japan) where language-school commissions genuinely cover service costs — and, tellingly, because it explains its own business model openly as trust marketing. Taisha, the community-turned-agency acquired at a RMB 700–800M valuation, shows the same money-logic ending in the industry's most famous collapse: listed-parent profit targets pushed prepay sales past delivery capacity until clients mid-application were stranded. 这道算术从来就不成立。留学申请是低频、高风险的信任型消费：没人买第二次，所以获客成本降不下来；每个学生的佣金有上限；而「免费」在结构上就等于把学生导向付佣金的大学——申请人最终闻出了这股味道。例外恰恰证明了规律：芥末留学能盈利地活下来，是因为它只占一条走廊（日本），语言学校的佣金在那里真的覆盖得住服务成本——而且它把自己的商业模式公开讲清楚，当作信任营销。太傻，那个以 7–8 亿元估值被上市公司收购的「社区转中介」，则演示了同一套资本逻辑的结局：对赌式的利润指标把预付费销售推到远超交付能力，最终申请到一半的客户被集体抛下——成为行业最著名的崩塌。

The graveyard's gift to AcademiaOne墓园留给 AcademiaOne 的遗产

A decade and roughly US$150M of other people's money established three facts a boutique should engrave: (1) client-paid independence is the resilient model — commission-funded "free" caps quality and corrupts advice, and applicants now know it; (2) information is a funnel, not a product — ApplySquare's users consumed the free database and bought judgment elsewhere, so publish data generously and sell advisory; (3) a trust business scales at the speed of delivery capacity, not sales — every player that let capital set the growth rate died of the gap. AcademiaOne never has to run these experiments.十年时间、约 1.5 亿美元的别人的钱，为精品机构刻下三条事实：（1）客户付费的独立模式才是抗周期的——佣金养的「免费」既封死了质量上限也污染了建议，而且申请人现在都知道了；（2）信息是漏斗，不是产品——申请方的用户消费完免费数据库，转身去别处购买判断力；所以要慷慨地公开数据、出售咨询；（3）信任生意的扩张速度只能等于交付能力的扩张速度——凡是让资本决定增速的玩家，都死在了这道缺口里。AcademiaOne 不需要再做一遍这些实验。

Why They Die死亡样本：失败模式

Taisha died with an A-share listing. ShunShun died of its own partner model. The platforms died of free. A ranked autopsy of the ten deadliest failure patterns — each one a design constraint for AcademiaOne. 太傻带着A股壳死了，顺顺死于自己的合伙人制，平台们死于「免费」。本章按致死率排序解剖十大失败模式——每一条都是 AcademiaOne 的设计约束。

What kills study-abroad companies is almost never a competitor, and almost never demand. It is structure. 太傻 Taisha — twenty years of forum-built trust — was sold to A-share Huawen Media for ~RMB 780M with a profit VAM; the earn-out converted a trust business into a sales-quota machine, prepaid client fees became group working capital, and when the parent's funding chain broke in 2019, twenty years of brand equity protected nobody. 顺顺 ShunShun promised to liberate consultants with equity partnerships and discovered the moat belonged to the partners, not the firm — they left with their client books when the paper stopped appreciating. 天道 Tiandao, the 2010s' premium Masters brand, kept selling prepaid packages into insolvency — a family paid RMB 128k weeks before it folded owing RMB 14.23M in refunds. 再来人 Valeon died in December 2025 of arithmetic: sub-30% gross margins on part-time celebrity mentors. 藤门 IvyGate died of unverifiable "ex-admissions-officer" prestige stacked on million-RMB multi-year prepayments. The free-platform cohort died of CAC exceeding commissions. And the 2024–26 policy shock proved that even blue chips die the same way — IDP, ApplyBoard, Adventus and the pathway providers were one correlated bet on Anglosphere visa politics. 杀死留学公司的几乎从来不是竞争对手，也几乎从来不是需求。是结构。太傻——二十年论坛攒下的信任——以约 7.8 亿元卖给 A 股华闻传媒并背上业绩对赌；对赌把一门信任生意改造成销售指标机器，学员预付费成了集团运营资金，2019 年母公司资金链断裂时，二十年的品牌没能保护任何人。顺顺许诺用股权合伙解放顾问，结果发现护城河属于合伙人而不属于公司——纸面财富停止升值时，他们带着客户名册离开了。2010 年代的高端硕士品牌天道，在资不抵债时仍在出售预付套餐——倒闭前几周还有家庭付了 12.8 万元，最终欠下 1423 万退费。再来人死于 2025 年 12 月，死因是一道算术题：兼职名校导师压出的不足 30% 毛利。藤门死于无法验证的「前招生官」光环叠加百万级多年预付款。免费平台一代死于获客成本超过佣金。而 2024–26 的政策冲击证明，蓝筹也是同一种死法——IDP、ApplyBoard、Adventus 和预科集团，都是压在英语国家签证政治上的同一笔相关性押注。

Ten design rules, engineered from the graveyard从墓园里反推出的十条设计规则

1. Never touch the float — ring-fence prepayments, milestone-recognise revenue. 2. Diversify policy, not just geography: no visa regime above ~40–55% of revenue. 3. Sell outcomes, price services — no guarantees, ever. 4. Pay for delivery, not signing. 5. Institutionalise the moat (playbooks, case libraries, 企业微信-held relationships) so nobody — including the founder — can walk out with the business. 6. Referrals over auctions: reputation is the only CAC that compounds. 7. Grow slower than quality: cap caseloads, hire ahead of demand. 8. Stay clean, loudly: auditable integrity is a commercial moat as scrutiny rises. 9. Manage seasonality like a bomb: 12-month runway, fixed costs sized to trough revenue. 10. Take patient money or none — every large Chinese agency death here has a capital-structure fingerprint.1. 永不动用浮存——预付款专户隔离，按里程碑确认收入。2. 分散的是政策而不只是地理：任何签证体系不超过营收的四到五成。3. 卖结果、按服务定价——永远不做担保。4. 为交付付薪，不为签单付薪。5. 把护城河制度化（方法论、案例库、企业微信沉淀的客户关系），让任何人——包括创始人——都带不走这门生意。6. 转介绍优于竞价：口碑是唯一能复利的获客成本。7. 增长慢于质量：限制个案数，先招人后接单。8. 干干净净，并且大声说出来：审查收紧的年代，可审计的操守就是商业护城河。9. 像拆弹一样管理季节性：12 个月现金跑道，固定成本按淡季配置。10. 只拿耐心资本，否则不拿——这里每一家大型中国机构的死亡都带着资本结构的指纹。

Pathways & the B2B Machine预科与B2B体系

Shorelight, INTO, Navitas, Study Group: the B2B machine universities use to buy enrolment. It is consolidating under PE ownership and shedding staff — and it shapes which universities need agents at all. Shorelight、INTO、Navitas、Study Group：大学用来「购买生源」的 B2B 机器。它们正在 PE 手中整合、裁员——也决定着哪些大学根本还需要中介。

Universities do not just receive students; they buy them — through a B2B machine of pathway colleges, enrolment outsourcers, rankings houses and SaaS rails. That machine is consolidating painfully. INTO lost £20.1M in FY24/25 and is fighting a former university partner in court; Study Group was passed through four private-equity owners before a 2026 distressed sale; Navitas — the most resilient operator — still needed a US$150M top-up from its PE owner; only Kaplan, owned by patient family-influenced capital (Graham Holdings), sails on calmly, selling its very boringness ("financial stability, 20-year partner tenures") as the product. The capital-light layer tells the opposite story: Studyportals (~$5M ever raised) became the sector's de facto data authority; QS — the kingmaker whose rankings are hard-wired into Chinese employer screening and even Shanghai hukou policy — prints money selling services to the universities it ranks. 大学不只是接收学生，它们「购买」学生——通过一台由预科学院、招生外包商、排名机构和 SaaS 基础设施组成的 B2B 机器。这台机器正在痛苦地整合。INTO 在 2024/25 财年亏损 2010 万英镑，还在和前合作大学对簿公堂；Study Group 被四任私募股权股东转手后，于 2026 年折价出售；最有韧性的 Navitas 也需要 PE 股东追加 1.5 亿美元补血；只有背靠耐心家族资本（Graham Holdings）的 Kaplan 波澜不惊，把自己的「无聊」（财务稳健、二十年合作关系）当作卖点。轻资产层则讲着相反的故事：Studyportals（历史融资仅约 500 万美元）成了行业事实上的数据权威；QS——排名被写进中国雇主筛选逻辑甚至上海落户政策的「册封者」——一边给大学排名，一边向被排名的大学卖服务，稳定地印着钞票。

For AcademiaOne this chapter is less about competition than about operations and leverage. Enroly now processes CAS issuance for roughly half of UK universities — mastering its document, financial-evidence and credibility-interview workflows is a concrete, sellable service edge. UniQuest built a business on one observation: universities answer applicants too slowly, and responding in minutes converts — the same speed-to-lead discipline applies to a boutique's WeChat funnel. Unibuddy and The Ambassador Platform (sold to IDP for just £9M) prove peer voices convert better than any brochure — a mechanism to steal operationally with an alumni-ambassador bench, not to buy. And the rankings duopoly defines the demand: Chinese Masters clients buy QS rank; structure school lists, tiers and case studies around rank bands rather than fighting it. 对 AcademiaOne 而言，这一章的意义不在竞争，而在运营与杠杆。Enroly 如今处理着大约一半英国大学的 CAS 发放——吃透它的材料、资金证明与可信度面试流程，是一项具体的、可以写进服务承诺的专业优势。UniQuest 的整个生意建立在一个观察上：大学回复申请人太慢，而「几分钟内响应」就能提升转化——同样的「响应速度」纪律完全适用于精品机构的微信漏斗。Unibuddy 和以区区 900 万英镑卖给 IDP 的 The Ambassador Platform 证明：同伴的声音比任何宣传册都更能转化——这是一个应该在运营上「偷走」的机制（建一支校友大使队伍），而不是去购买的产品。排名双头垄断则定义了需求本身：中国硕士客户买的就是 QS 排名；选校清单、定价档位和案例都应围绕排名区间来组织，而不是与之对抗。

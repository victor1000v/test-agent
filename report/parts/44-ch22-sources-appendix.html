Keeping this atlas alive. Competitive intelligence decays fast in this market — Valeon went from "industry benchmark" to defunct within twelve months. Three habits keep the picture current at near-zero cost: re-run the failure watchlist quarterly (wage-arrear posts on 脉脉/BOSS直聘 are the earliest death signal, as Valeon showed); re-check 黑猫投诉 counts for the ten closest competitors each season; and log every competitor mention that clients bring to sales calls — prospects are the best free intelligence network in the industry. The underlying dataset ships with this report as machine-readable JSON, so each refresh is an edit, not a rebuild. 让这份地图保持鲜活。这个市场的竞争情报衰减得很快——再来人从「行业标杆」到倒闭只用了十二个月。三个几乎零成本的习惯能让图景保持最新：每季度重跑一遍失败观察名单（脉脉/BOSS直聘上的欠薪爆料是最早的死亡信号，再来人已经示范过）；每个申请季复查十家最近竞争对手的黑猫投诉量；把客户在咨询电话里提到的每一个竞品都记录下来——潜在客户是这个行业最好的免费情报网。本报告随附机器可读的 JSON 数据集，每次更新只是一次编辑，而不是一次重建。

Marketing & CAC in China获客与营销经济学

Where Chinese Masters applicants actually come from in 2026: RED, Zhihu, private-domain WeChat funnels, referrals — and what a signed client costs at each door. The chapter AcademiaOne’s budget should be built on. 2026 年，中国硕士申请客户到底从哪里来：小红书、知乎、企业微信私域、转介绍——以及每扇门后签下一个客户的真实成本。AcademiaOne 的预算应该建立在这一章之上。

Three structural facts decide who can afford to exist in this market. First, the decision journey moved to 小红书: for the core Masters applicant — a 20–24-year-old researching for herself — discovery begins with XHS searches like 「英国一年硕」 and 「留学中介避雷」 months before any consultation; Baidu now captures parents and the 低龄 segment. Second, lead costs inflated everywhere as thousands of micro-studios crowded the same formats, spawning an arms race of undeclared 素人矩阵 account farms — and platform crackdowns that can zero a matrix (and collaterally ban the linked 机构号) overnight. Third, trust collapsed faster than prices: after the 保录取 scandals and the 跑路 wave, the highest-converting asset in the category is verifiable proof — named cases, transparent contracts, refund clarity — not reach. Every number below is an estimate-range to be re-validated with small test budgets; the ordering between channels is the robust finding. 三个结构性事实决定了谁在这个市场里活得起。第一，决策路径已迁移到小红书：核心硕士申请人——一个自己做研究的 20–24 岁学生——在任何咨询发生之前几个月，就从「英国一年硕」「留学中介避雷」这样的搜索开始了；百度如今抓住的是家长和低龄段。第二，所有渠道的线索成本都在通胀：数千家微型工作室挤进同样的内容格式，催生了未报备素人矩阵的军备竞赛——而平台的整治浪潮能让一个矩阵一夜归零（还可能连坐封掉关联机构号）。第三，信任崩塌的速度快过价格：保录取丑闻与跑路潮之后，这个品类里转化率最高的资产是可验证的证据——实名案例、透明合同、清晰退款——而不是触达量。下文所有数字都是需要用小额投放重新校准的估算区间；真正稳健的结论是渠道之间的排序。

The channel economics sort into three tiers. Compounding owned channels: referral (top boutiques take 40–70% of signings here, at RMB 500–2,000 per signed client and 50–80% close rates — engineered through offer-day rituals and formal asks at the two peak-gratitude moments); 知乎 long-form with a content half-life measured in years; and the 企业微信 private-domain SOP that converts everything (and keeps the client book corporate when consultants leave). Workable paid: XHS 聚光 search on brand and competitor-adjacent terms (est. RMB 100–400 per lead form), 薯条 testing at RMB 100–300 per note, declared 蒲公英 KOL buys. Structurally wrong for a boutique: Baidu SEM (est. RMB 5,000–15,000 per signed client, parent-facing) and 抖音 livestream funnels (RMB 9.9 lead courses feeding telesales floors at single-digit close rates — mega-agency economics). The compliance line is bright: 广告法 Article 24 bans express or implied admission guarantees, with fines in the RMB 100k–1M band — structure refunds contractually, never advertise outcomes. 渠道经济学分成三层。可复利的自有渠道：转介绍（头部工作室 40–70% 的签约来自于此，单客成本约 500–2,000 元、签约率 50–80%——通过 offer 日仪式感和在两个感恩峰值时点的正式请求来「工程化」）；内容半衰期以年计的知乎长文；以及承接一切转化的企业微信私域 SOP（顾问离职时，客户资产留在公司）。可用的付费渠道：小红书聚光在品牌词与竞品相邻词上的搜索投放（表单成本估计 100–400 元）、每篇 100–300 元的薯条测试、报备的蒲公英 KOL 合作。对精品机构结构性错误的渠道：百度 SEM（签约成本估计 5,000–15,000 元，面向家长）和抖音直播漏斗（9.9 元引流课喂给电销团队，签约率个位数——那是大机构的经济学）。合规红线清晰明亮：广告法第 24 条禁止明示或暗示的录取保证，罚款区间约 10 万–100 万元——退款条款写进合同即可，永远不要拿结果做广告。

The year-one acquisition architecture第一年的获客架构

At an indicative RMB 15–30k/month budget: 50% content engine — one flagship 机构号 + a founder account posting 4–7 notes/week (offer 晒单 in Nov–Mar, 案例复盘, 避雷 — the highest-engagement format), plus two pillar 知乎 answers a month, everything repurposed into 视频号 clips; 20% 聚光 search and brand defence, validated at RMB 100–400/lead before scaling; 10% declared micro-KOL tests; 20% the referral programme (RMB 800–1,500 rewards, formal asks at offer day and visa day, incentivised alumni 申请总结 seeded into 知乎 search). Zero Baidu SEM beyond brand terms, zero Douyin livestreams, zero undeclared matrices, zero bought reviews. Target: blended CAC under RMB 2,500 against a RMB 50k ticket — 5% of revenue where mega-agencies burn 15–25% — reaching 50%+ referral-and-organic signings within 18 months.以每月 1.5–3 万元的指导性预算：50% 给内容引擎——一个旗舰机构号 + 创始人账号每周 4–7 篇笔记（11–3 月 offer 晒单、案例复盘、避雷——互动率最高的格式），每月两篇支柱型知乎长文，全部内容二次剪辑进视频号；20% 给聚光搜索与品牌词防守，先以 100–400 元/表单验证再放量；10% 给报备的微型 KOL 测试；20% 给转介绍体系（800–1,500 元奖励、offer 日与获签日的正式请求、有激励的校友申请总结沉淀进知乎搜索）。百度 SEM 除品牌词外为零，抖音直播为零，未报备矩阵为零，买评价为零。目标：在 5 万元客单价下把综合获客成本压到 2,500 元以内——营收占比 5%，而大机构烧 15–25%——并在 18 个月内让转介绍+自然流量占签约的一半以上。

Chapter 19 · Part V第五部 · 第 19 章

Patterns & Insights规律与洞察

Fourteen patterns that hold across a hundred-plus companies, two languages and four geographies. This is the chapter to reread before every major decision. 十四条在一百多家公司、两种语言、四大地域中反复成立的规律。每次重大决策之前，值得重读的就是这一章。

Individual companies lie; populations don't. When 141 companies are read side by side, a small number of laws keep asserting themselves across languages, geographies and price tiers. These fourteen are the ones sturdy enough to build a company on. 单个公司会说谎，群体不会。把 141 家公司并排阅读，少数几条规律会跨越语言、地域和价格档，反复自我证明。以下十四条，坚固到可以把一家公司建在上面。

01

Trust attaches to people, not firms信任附着于个人，而非机构

ShunShun's partner platform, the XHS studio boom and 启德's "no founder story" weakness all prove it: in high-stakes admissions, clients trust a named, checkable person. TAL's brand transferred zero trust to ShunShun. Build founder-fronted IP on institutional rails.顺顺的合伙人平台、小红书工作室的爆发、启德「没有创始人故事」的战略软肋都证明：高风险的申请决策中，客户信任的是可查证的具体的人。好未来的品牌没能给顺顺转移一分信任。要在机构化的轨道上建创始人 IP。

02

Prepayment float is the hidden leverage — and the death mode预付款浮存是隐性杠杆，也是死亡模式

天道 (RMB 14.23M owed), 太傻, Valeon and IvyGate died identically: prepaid fees spent against fixed costs, no escrow, collapse when signings slowed. Milestone billing with ring-fenced client money is a category-first differentiator.天道（欠退费 1423 万）、太傻、再来人、藤门死法完全相同：预付费被固定成本消耗，无任何托管，新签放缓即崩盘。里程碑计费 + 客户资金专户隔离，是全品类首创级的差异化。

03

No VC-funded Chinese consumer player became a durable winner中国赛道无一家 VC 消费级公司成为耐久赢家

Valeon dead; 51offer zombified; myOffer plateaued; ApplySquare at 13 staff; ShunShun absorbed. The healthiest operators — Compass, mbaMission, Jiemo, Studyportals — are all bootstrapped. Capital buys traffic, never trust.再来人死亡；51offer 僵尸化；myOffer 停滞；申请方剩 13 人；顺顺被收编。最健康的运营者——指南者、mbaMission、芥末、Studyportals——全部自力更生。资本买得到流量，永远买不到信任。

04

Guarantees are debt, and they drift toward fraud担保即负债，且会滑向欺诈

太傻's refund guarantees became a liability avalanche; AdmitWrite drifted into RMB 1M+ guaranteed-admission fraud now documented in court records. Even the benign version corrupts school lists downward. The sane structure: contingent success payments, never refund promises.太傻的退款承诺变成负债雪崩；AdmitWrite 滑向百万级保录欺诈，如今写在法院判决书里。即使良性版本也会让选校保守化。理性结构是：或有成功付款，永远不做退款承诺。

05

Part-time mentor arbitrage breaks at premium prices兼职导师套利在高价位崩坏

GGU: ~15 insured staff behind RMB 80–100k fees and a notorious complaint thread. Valeon: mentor costs crushed gross margin below 30% and killed it. The fix is proven twice — Stoooges' top-of-market full-timers and Compass's all-employee pods.世毕盟：8–10 万收费背后约 15 名参保员工和一条著名的控诉长帖。再来人：导师成本把毛利压到 30% 以下并致死。解法被验证过两次——三士渡的顶薪全职与指南者的全员工小组。

06

Price transparency is an unused weapon in the premium tier价格透明是高端档无人使用的武器

Every Chinese premium boutique hides pricing, and hidden pricing correlates with "sign-then-downgrade" complaints. Compass publishes and owns the healthiest reputation at scale; the whole MBA cluster has published rates for decades. First transparent premium operator wins the tier.中国高端精品全部藏价，而藏价与「签约后降级」投诉高度相关。指南者公开定价并坐拥规模玩家中最健康的口碑；整个 MBA 集群明码标价数十年。第一个透明的高端玩家将赢下这个档位。

07

The channel stack inverted twice: SEM → 知乎 → 小红书渠道栈两次倒转：竞价 → 知乎 → 小红书

Era 1 was Baidu + branches + CCTV; Era 2 was Zhihu long-form authority; Era 3 is XHS discovery with founder faces and offer walls. Brands trapped in yesterday's channel (和创, 毕达, BeBeyond) became invisible to buyers within one student generation — about three years.第一代是百度+门店+央视；第二代是知乎长文权威；第三代是小红书发现层——创始人出镜与 offer 墙。困在上一代渠道的品牌（和创、毕达、BeBeyond）在一个学生世代（约三年）内就从买家视野里消失了。

08

Astroturf now backfires刷评软文已开始反噬

PEC's self-authored rankings, Shinyway's advertorial wall, Quad's SEO farms and 芝士圈's alleged review deletion are all recognised and held against the brands. Durable trust assets are third-party and verifiable: GMAT Club reviews, public order ledgers, named client endorsements.优越的自产排名、新通的软文墙、Quad 的 SEO 农场、芝士圈被指删差评——都已被识破并被反向计入品牌负分。耐久的信任资产是第三方且可验证的：GMAT Club 评价、公开订单账本、实名客户代言。

09

Commission-volume models are leveraged bets on visa politics佣金流量模式是对签证政治的杠杆押注

2024–26 ran the experiment: IDP -~90% from peak, ApplyBoard written down >80%, Adventus dead owing its own agents. "Destination diversification" inside commission economics is one correlated trade. Student-paid fees across UK+HK+SG is the architecture that survives.2024–26 做完了实验：IDP 较峰值跌约九成，ApplyBoard 减记超 80%，Adventus 倒闭还欠着自己代理的佣金。佣金经济内的「目的地多元化」是同一笔相关性交易。英港新三线的学生付费模式才是穿越周期的架构。

10

Refund architecture is the #1 complaint — design it as product退费架构是头号投诉——把它设计成产品

The single most frequent problem across all 141 companies: refund disputes and predatory clauses. This is not a legal detail; it is the market's largest unmet emotional need. Staged payments, defined exits and fast early refunds convert the industry's worst liability into a loudest selling point.141 家公司里最高频的问题：退费纠纷与霸王条款。这不是法务细节，而是市场最大的未被满足的情感需求。分段付款、明确退出节点、早期快速退款，能把行业最大负债变成最响亮卖点。

11

The squeezed middle dies; health lives at the barbell ends中间层被挤死；健康在杠铃两端

Mid-size generalists — Wiseway, Jinqiao, Wanjia, IEC Abroad — decay slowly: no scale advantage, no boutique intimacy, no digital-native acquisition. What thrives: transparent factories (Compass) or capped senior boutiques (Menlo, Stoooges). Pick an end and resist drifting to the middle.中型综合机构——威久、津桥、万佳、IEC Abroad——在缓慢腐烂：无规模优势、无精品亲密度、无数字原生获客。活得好的是两端：透明工厂（指南者）或限额资深精品（Menlo、三士渡）。选定一端，抵抗向中间漂移。

12

US-only killed the last premium generation; UK+HK+SG is the resilient book单押美国杀死了上一代高端机构；英港新才是韧性组合

天道, 啄木鸟, 藤门, Topsedu, 和创, 智慧 all bet on US admissions; post-2020 shocks broke or endangered every one. Chinese demand migrated to one-year UK/HK/SG Masters — but UK-only is also a single trade. UK-led with real HK/SG lines, US as a premium option.天道、啄木鸟、藤门、托普仕、和创、智慧全部押注美国；2020 年后的冲击摧毁或重创了每一家。中国需求迁移到英港新一年制硕士——但纯英国也是单一押注。以英国为主线、港新为实线、美国为高端选项。

13

PhD advising is structurally unserved whitespace博士申请是结构性无主空白

Compass has "almost no PhD capability"; the mentor shops that served research applicants (GGU, Valeon) are dead or damaged. UK/HK supervisor-outreach PhD advising needs exactly the senior same-field judgment a boutique can staff and a factory cannot — premium pricing, near-zero credible competition.指南者「几乎没有博士能力」；曾服务研究型申请者的导师制机构（世毕盟、再来人）已死或受损。英港博士的套磁式申请恰恰需要精品能配置、工厂配不出的资深同领域判断——高溢价，几乎无可信对手。

14

Verifiable modest numbers beat unverifiable perfect ones可验证的普通数字胜过不可验证的完美数字

澳际 claims 700+ consultants, insures 359. Topsedu claims 99%. Only two of 141 companies externally audit outcomes (Solomon, ARINGO). In a market where every number is assumed false, an audited modest number is a category weapon.澳际宣称 700+ 顾问，参保 359 人。托普仕宣称 99%。141 家公司中只有两家外部审计自己的结果（Solomon、ARINGO）。在一个所有数字都被默认为假的市场里，一个经过审计的普通数字就是品类级武器。

The Money Map资本地图

Roughly $2 billion of disclosed venture and PE money has flowed through this database of companies. Mapping who raised what against what happened next yields an uncomfortable, liberating conclusion. 这份数据库里的公司，累计流过约 20 亿美元的已披露风险与私募资本。把「谁融了多少钱」和「后来发生了什么」放在一张图上，结论令人不适，却也令人解脱。

Add up the disclosed capital that has flowed through this database — the VC rounds, the buyouts, the IPOs, the acquisition considerations — and the total comfortably clears US$2 billion. Now look at what it bought. In the Chinese consumer cohort, the record is perfect and damning: not one VC-funded consumer study-abroad company became a durable independent winner. Valeon is dead. 51offer is a zombie on old SEO. myOffer plateaued at Series B. ApplySquare shrank to 13 employees. ShunShun was absorbed and marginalised. Xiaozhan missed its IPO window and retreated to a niche. Globally the same curve: ApplyBoard written down >80%, Adventus in administration, Shorelight carrying credit-loss allowances, Puxin's US$1.7B listing now an OTC shell — while Crimson, the lone compounding premium unicorn, is the exception that clarifies the rule: its consulting core was cash-generative early and it raised strikingly little for its scale. 把这份数据库里所有已披露的资本加总——VC 轮次、并购、IPO、收购对价——总额轻松超过 20 亿美元。再看看这些钱买到了什么。在中国消费级赛道，记录完美而残酷：没有一家 VC 投资的消费级留学公司成为持久的独立赢家。再来人死了。51offer 靠旧 SEO 苟活。myOffer 停在 B 轮。申请方缩到 13 人。顺顺被收编、被边缘化。小站错过 IPO 窗口、退守利基。全球曲线相同：ApplyBoard 减记超 80%，Adventus 进入破产管理，Shorelight 背着信用损失拨备，朴新 17 亿美元的上市公司如今是 OTC 空壳——而唯一持续复利的高端独角兽 Crimson，恰是那个证明规律的例外：它的咨询主业很早就自我造血，融资额相对规模少得惊人。

Meanwhile the healthiest operators in all 141 are bootstrapped: Compass grew self-funded to 500+ staff and 1.16M registered users; mbaMission compounded for 27 years into the consolidator of its category; Menlo, Stacy Blackman, ARINGO, 芝士圈, Jiemo (profitable since 2017) and Studyportals (~$5M raised, deliberately) never needed the money. The mechanism is not mysterious. Admissions advising is a trust purchase with no network effects — capital buys traffic, never trust; delivery is senior-labour-intensive, so gross margin compresses exactly when volume grows; growth targets institutionalise over-promising (太傻's earn-out built its fatal refund machine); and the acquisition curse is near-universal — nearly every consultancy bought by a listed parent (太傻/华闻, 啄木鸟/朴新, 顺顺/好未来, 环球雅思/Pearson→朴新) was damaged or destroyed by the parent's incentives or collapse. 与此同时，141 家公司里最健康的运营者全部自力更生：指南者零融资做到 500+ 员工、116 万注册用户；mbaMission 复利 27 年成为品类整合者；Menlo、Stacy Blackman、ARINGO、芝士圈、芥末（2017 年起盈利）、Studyportals（刻意只融了约 500 万美元）从来不需要那笔钱。机制并不神秘：留学咨询是一桩没有网络效应的信任型购买——资本买得到流量，永远买不到信任；交付依赖资深人力，规模一涨毛利就被压缩；增长指标会把过度承诺制度化（太傻的对赌造出了它致命的退费销售机器）；而「被收购魔咒」近乎普遍——几乎每一家被上市母公司买走的咨询机构（太傻/华闻、啄木鸟/朴新、顺顺/好未来、环球雅思/培生→朴新）都被母公司的激励或崩盘所伤、所毁。

AcademiaOne's capital doctrineAcademiaOne 的资本守则

Bootstrap the consulting core — its economics fund themselves when quality holds, and the discipline is a strategic advantage, not a constraint. If capital is ever taken, only patient money with no VAM, no earn-outs, no growth deadlines; every large Chinese agency death in this atlas carries a capital-structure fingerprint. And treat acquisition offers from larger education groups as the risk they historically are: in this database, selling to a listed parent has been an exit for the founder and a death sentence for the brand — with mbaMission's succession-driven purchase of Accepted showing the one clean alternative: build the institution well enough that you become the consolidator, on your own timeline.咨询主业坚持自筹——只要质量守得住，它的经济模型自己养活自己；这种纪律是战略优势而非限制。若有一天真要引入资本，只拿没有对赌、没有业绩承诺、没有增长死线的耐心钱；这份图集里每一家大型中国机构的死亡都带着资本结构的指纹。对更大教育集团的收购邀约，要按它在历史上的真实面目对待：在这个数据库里，卖给上市母公司往往是创始人的退出、品牌的死刑——而 mbaMission 因对手创始人退休而完成的收购展示了唯一体面的替代路径：把机构建设得足够好，让你自己成为整合者，按你自己的时间表。

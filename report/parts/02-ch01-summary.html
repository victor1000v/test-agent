Executive Summary执行摘要

The whole atlas in eight minutes: what the market looks like, who actually makes money, why most challengers fail, and the five moves that matter for AcademiaOne. 八分钟读完整份报告：市场格局如何、谁真正赚钱、为什么大多数挑战者失败，以及对 AcademiaOne 真正重要的五步棋。

AcademiaOne is positioned in the strongest corridor of the market at the strongest moment of its cycle, inside an industry whose structure punishes almost everyone else in this report. That is the finding, and the rest of this atlas is the evidence. AcademiaOne 正处在整个市场最强的走廊、最强的周期时点上，而这个行业的结构惩罚着本报告里几乎所有其他玩家。这是结论——图集的其余部分都是证据。

The market. Chinese outbound study has recovered to roughly pre-pandemic scale, but its geography has been violently reshuffled: the US is down ~29% from peak and politically radioactive; the UK remains the default Masters destination; Hong Kong and Singapore are absorbing the overflow at 30–50% application growth per cycle. The Masters degree is the centre of gravity — 70–80% of stated intent, ~90,000 Chinese postgraduates in UK universities alone — and the 考研 collapse (registrations down 27% in three years) keeps feeding the corridor. The advisory industry around this demand was deregulated in 2017 and has shattered into 80,000+ registered companies where no one holds double-digit share and trust is the scarcest commodity. 市场。中国出国留学已恢复到疫情前规模，但版图被猛烈洗牌：美国较峰值下跌约 29% 且政治上高危；英国仍是默认硕士目的地；香港与新加坡以每季 30–50% 的申请增速承接溢出。硕士是绝对重心——留学意向的七到八成，仅英国就有约 9 万在读中国研究生——而考研塌方（报名三年降 27%）持续向这条走廊输送客源。围绕这些需求的咨询行业在 2017 年被取消资质管制后，碎成 8 万多家注册公司，无人握有两位数份额，信任是最稀缺的商品。

The competition. The mega-agencies still own the largest share and the worst complaint records — refund traps, consultant churn, commission steering. The premium tier just lost its benchmark: Valeon collapsed in December 2025 on sub-30% gross margins, joining 天道, 太傻 and 藤门 in the graveyard of prepay-funded premium brands. The healthiest scaled operator, Compass (指南者), industrialised transparency at RMB 18–24k with junior full-time staff — and cannot serve depth. The fastest-growing species, the 小红书-native studio, converts founder personas into RMB 8–50k packages with zero institutional durability. Globally, the 2024–26 visa shocks halved or killed the commission-volume giants (IDP −~90% from peak, ApplyBoard written down >80%, Adventus dead), while the bootstrapped MBA boutiques — published prices, full-time consultants, audited outcomes — compounded through it all untouched. Across 141 companies, one pattern repeats: no VC-funded Chinese consumer study-abroad company ever became a durable independent winner; every collapse carries a capital-structure or trust-structure fingerprint. 竞争。传统大所仍占最大份额，也背着最难看的投诉记录——退款陷阱、顾问流失、佣金导向。高端档刚刚失去它的标杆：再来人 2025 年 12 月倒在不足 30% 的毛利上，与天道、太傻、藤门一起躺进「预付费高端品牌」的墓园。规模化玩家中最健康的指南者，用全职初级团队在 1.8–2.4 万档把透明工业化了——但做不了深度。增长最快的物种，小红书原生工作室，把创始人人设变成 8 千–5 万的套餐——但零机构延续性。放眼全球，2024–26 年签证冲击把佣金流量巨头腰斩或杀死（IDP 较峰值约 -90%，ApplyBoard 减记超 80%，Adventus 倒闭），而自筹资金的 MBA 精品——明码标价、全职顾问、审计成果——安然复利穿越了这一切。141 家公司中，同一个规律反复出现：没有一家 VC 投资的中国消费级留学公司成为持久的独立赢家；每一次崩塌都带着资本结构或信任结构的指纹。

The opening. Between Compass's 24k transparent factory and the discredited 100k+ mentor tier sits a thinly-held band — roughly RMB 40–80k — where nobody offers what the evidence says wins: published prices, named senior full-time consultants with hard caseload caps, milestone billing with ring-fenced client money, genuine UK-side delivery, zero university commissions, and verifiable outcomes. Every element is proven separately somewhere in this report. No company among 141 has assembled them. 机会。在指南者 2.4 万的透明工厂与信誉扫地的 10 万+ 导师档之间，横着一条几乎无人坚守的价格带——约 4–8 万元——没有人在这里提供证据显示能赢的组合：公开定价、署名资深全职顾问加硬性接案上限、里程碑计费加客户资金专户、真实的英国本土交付、零院校佣金、可验证的成果。每个要素都在本报告的某处被单独验证过。141 家公司中没有一家完成了组装。

The five moves, in order五步棋，按顺序

1. Take the vacant tier: published RMB 40–80k pricing, named senior full-time delivery, 35-case caps, zero commissions — the credible-premium UK/HK/SG specialist (Ch. 7, 20).占领空档：公开的 4–8 万定价、署名资深全职交付、35 案上限、零佣金——英港新「可信高端」专家（第 7、20 章）。

2. Make refund-safety a product: milestone billing (30/40/30) with client money ring-fenced — the anti-天道 architecture nobody offers (Ch. 18, 19).把退费安全做成产品：30/40/30 里程碑计费 + 客户资金专户——无人提供的「反天道」架构（第 18、19 章）。

3. Run founder-fronted 小红书 on institutional rails: the studios' transparency assets, with the client book held in 企业微信 by the company (Ch. 10, 16).创始人出镜的小红书 + 机构化轨道：学工作室的透明资产，客户资产由公司沉淀在企业微信（第 10、16 章）。

4. Publish verifiable outcomes from client #1: an audited-methodology annual report in a market where every number is assumed false (Ch. 14, 19).从第一位客户起公布可验证成果：在一个所有数字都被默认为假的市场里，发布方法论透明的年度报告（第 14、19 章）。

5. Bootstrap, cap, and diversify policy risk: no VC, no guarantees, no destination above ~55% of revenue; add the PhD line by month six — the unowned whitespace (Ch. 17, 20, 21).自筹、限额、分散政策风险：不融 VC、不做担保、单一目的地不超营收约 55%；第六个月上线博士线——那片无主空白（第 17、20、21 章）。

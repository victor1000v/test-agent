The Competitive Map竞争地图

One hundred and thirty companies collapse into a small number of repeating machines. Learn the archetypes and every competitor becomes legible — including the ones that will be founded next year. 一百三十多家公司，归根结底是少数几种反复出现的「机器」。看懂这些原型，每个对手都变得可读——包括明年才会成立的那些。

Read side by side, the 141 companies in this atlas collapse into nine repeating machines. Each archetype has its own economics, its own defining flaw, and its own lesson — and every competitor AcademiaOne will ever meet, including ones not yet founded, will be running one of them. 并排阅读，本图集的 141 家公司归结为九种反复出现的机器。每种原型都有自己的经济学、自己的致命缺陷、自己的教训——AcademiaOne 将来遇到的每一个对手，包括尚未成立的那些，都在运行其中一种。

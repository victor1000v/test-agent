The Mega-Agencies传统大所

New Oriental, EIC, JJL, Aoji, Shinyway: the billion-RMB machines that industrialised study abroad. They are slower and more distrusted than ever — and still take the largest share of the market. Understanding why is the first lesson in this business. 新东方前途、启德、金吉列、澳际、新通：把留学做成流水线的十亿级机器。它们比以往任何时候都更迟缓、更不被信任——却仍占据最大的市场份额。理解「为什么」，是这门生意的第一课。

The traditional giants are still the market. New Oriental's study-abroad consulting arm alone booked an estimated US$700–850M in FY2024–25 — more than every boutique in this report combined — and EIC, JJL, Shinyway and New Channel each run branch networks with one to three thousand staff. Their machine is simple: brand age plus physical branches plus paid search buys the trust of parents, a commissioned sales floor converts it, and a production line of junior consultants delivers it. It works, and it is precisely why they are vulnerable: every part of the machine optimises for signing, not outcomes. 传统巨头依然「就是市场本身」。仅新东方前途出国的留学咨询业务，2024–25 财年估计就有 7–8.5 亿美元收入——超过本报告所有精品机构的总和；启德、金吉列、新通、新航道各自都运营着一千到三千人的分支网络。它们的机器很简单：品牌年头 + 线下门店 + 百度投放买来家长的信任，提成制销售团队负责转化，初级顾问流水线负责交付。这套机器有效，但也正是它的软肋所在：机器的每个环节都为「签单」而优化，而不是为「结果」。

The complaint record is structural, not accidental. Across 黑猫投诉 and forum archives the same patterns repeat by design: refund clauses that keep 50% after delivering only a school list (New Oriental), branch-manager promises that die with staff turnover (EIC), service that collapses the day after signing (JJL, Bailitop), and university-commission models that quietly steer students to partner schools (JJL's 2,600+ partners, IDP, Aoji). Meanwhile Tiandao — the closest thing the 2010s had to a scaled premium Masters brand — collapsed in 2022 with RMB 14M+ in unrefunded prepaid fees, and JiaHua ShiDa shows what happens when a moat built on regulatory licences is deregulated away. 巨头的投诉记录是结构性的，不是偶然的。翻遍黑猫投诉与论坛档案，同样的模式反复出现：只交付了一份选校清单就扣留 50% 费用的退款条款（新东方）、随经办人离职一起消失的口头承诺（启德）、签约次日服务即断崖式下滑（金吉列、百利天下）、以及悄悄把学生导向返佣院校的代理模式（金吉列 2600+ 合作院校、IDP、澳际）。与此同时，天道——2010 年代最接近「规模化高端硕士品牌」的公司——在 2022 年带着 1400 万+ 未退学费崩塌；嘉华世达则示范了「建立在牌照上的护城河，会随牌照制度一起被取消」。

Their digital transition is the open flank. Every giant is an institutional brand with no founder IP, running bland multi-account matrices on 小红书 while their most damaging complaint threads sit unanswered on the exact forums (一亩三分地, 知乎) where Masters applicants do their diligence. They still own lower-tier-city parents and walk-in traffic; they are visibly losing the RED-native generation that AcademiaOne serves. 数字化转型是它们敞开的侧翼。每家巨头都是没有创始人 IP 的机构品牌，在小红书上运营着面目模糊的矩阵账号，而最伤品牌的投诉长帖，就躺在硕士申请人做尽调的那些论坛上（一亩三分地、知乎）无人回应。它们仍然握有低线城市家长和到店客流，但正在肉眼可见地失去小红书原生的一代——恰恰是 AcademiaOne 服务的人群。

What AcademiaOne takes from the giantsAcademiaOne 从巨头身上带走什么

Copy:学习： EIC's annual White Paper (own a proprietary data report for UK/HK Masters); New Oriental's full-cycle LTV thinking (application → arrival → career); Shinyway's regional density (own one city's channels before expanding); Bailitop's proof that Masters clients can be served nationally with no branches.启德的年度《白皮书》（做一份自有的英港硕士申请数据报告）；新东方的全周期 LTV 思维（申请 → 落地 → 职业）；新通的区域纵深（先吃透一座城市的渠道再扩张）；百利天下证明了硕士客户完全可以无门店、全国化交付。

Exploit:利用： every giant's refund-trap contracts, consultant churn and commission steering are documented in public — AcademiaOne's counter-positioning (no university commissions, named senior consultants end-to-end, milestone billing, capped intake) writes itself from their complaint threads.巨头的退款陷阱条款、顾问流失与返佣导向都有公开记录——AcademiaOne 的反向定位（不拿院校返佣、指名资深顾问全程负责、按里程碑收费、限量接单）几乎可以直接从它们的投诉帖里抄出来。

Never:绝不： let claimed numbers diverge from verifiable reality (Aoji's 700 claimed consultants vs 359 insured staff), buy fake ranking advertorials (Shinyway's 98.8% employment claims), or let prepaid fees become working capital (Tiandao's epitaph).让宣传数字脱离可验证的现实（澳际宣称 700+ 顾问，参保仅 359 人）；购买虚假排名软文（新通的「98.8% 就业率」）；把预收学费当作运营资金（这是天道的墓志铭）。

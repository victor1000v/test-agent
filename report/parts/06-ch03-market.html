The market is structurally healthy and geographically scrambled. Total outbound flow has recovered to roughly pre-pandemic levels — over a million Chinese tertiary students are enrolled abroad at any moment, the world's largest sending stock — but the destinations have been violently reshuffled. The US has fallen ~29% from its 2019/20 peak and been overtaken by India; the UK holds its position as the default Masters destination (peak 158,335 Chinese students in 2022/23) even as policy squeezes it — the Graduate Route proposed down to 18 months, a £925-per-student levy confirmed from 2028–29; and Hong Kong and Singapore are absorbing the overflow at explosive rates, with mainlanders making up 70–80% of Hong Kong's non-local students. Australia and Canada capped themselves out of the growth. The default client strategy is now 英港新联申 — simultaneous UK/HK/SG applications — which is precisely the corridor AcademiaOne serves. 这个市场结构性健康、地理上剧烈重构。出国总量已基本恢复到疫情前水平——任一时点都有超过一百万中国学生在海外高校就读，全球最大生源存量——但目的地被猛烈洗牌。美国较 2019/20 峰值下跌约 29%，被印度反超；英国守住「默认硕士目的地」的位置（2022/23 峰值 15.8 万中国学生），尽管政策在收紧——毕业生签证拟缩至 18 个月、2028–29 学年起确认征收每生 925 英镑；香港和新加坡以爆炸性速度承接溢出，香港非本地生中内地生占七到八成。澳大利亚和加拿大用配额把自己挡在了增长之外。客户的默认策略已经是「英港新联申」——恰好就是 AcademiaOne 服务的那条走廊。

Beneath the geography, three demand-side currents matter more than any single policy. The 考研 pressure valve is releasing — registrations down 1.3M in three years, and every January–March the industry now runs a standard 考研后留学 pivot season feeding HK/UK rounds still open. Employability logic rules school choice: with 12.22M graduates in 2025 and urban youth unemployment near 19%, QS rank bands drive decisions because employers, 选调生 lists and hukou policies (Shanghai: direct hukou for QS-top-50 graduates) key off them. And value-consciousness is structural: RMB weakness plus consumption downgrade favour one-year degrees near home. Meanwhile the industry serving this demand was deregulated in 2017 and shattered into 80,000+ registered companies with CR5 plausibly under 15% — an advisory market worth roughly RMB 30–50B a year in which no one owns double-digit share and trust is the scarcest commodity. 在地理版图之下，三股需求侧潮流比任何单一政策都更重要。考研泄压阀正在放气——报名三年少了 130 万人，每年 1–3 月，全行业都在跑标准化的「考研后留学」转化季，喂给尚未截止的港英申请轮次。就业逻辑主宰选校：2025 届毕业生 1222 万、城镇青年失业率近 19%，QS 排名区间之所以决定选择，是因为雇主、选调生名录和落户政策（上海：QS 前 50 毕业生直接落户）都以它为准。性价比意识则是结构性的：人民币走弱叠加消费降级，让「离家近的一年制学位」占尽优势。而服务这些需求的行业在 2017 年被取消资质管制后碎成了 8 万多家注册公司，前五名集中度大概率不足 15%——一个年规模约 300–500 亿元、无人握有两位数份额、信任是最稀缺商品的咨询市场。

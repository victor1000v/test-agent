The Market in Numbers市场数据全景

Before studying competitors, study the water they swim in: how many Chinese students go abroad, where they now go, how much of the flow is Masters degrees, and what the advisory market around them is worth. 研究对手之前，先研究他们所处的水域：每年多少中国学生出国、现在流向哪里、其中硕士占多大比重、围绕他们的服务市场值多少钱。

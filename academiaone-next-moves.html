05The thesis核心论点

One square is empty. We take it.有一个方格空着。我们去占。

Across all 141 companies, nobody combines these six properties. Each one is the inverse of a documented failure mode:141 家公司中，没有一家同时具备下面六个属性。每一个都是某种有据可查的死亡模式的反面：

Published prices公开定价

Hidden pricing correlates with "sign-then-downgrade" complaints across every Chinese premium shop.藏价与「签约后降级」投诉在每家中国高端机构身上高度相关。

Senior, full-time, capped资深 · 全职 · 限额

Part-time mentor networks broke GGU and killed Valeon. Full-time delivery is the only premium quality moat.兼职导师网络拖垮世毕盟、杀死再来人。全职交付是高价位唯一可靠的质量护城河。

Milestone billing, ring-fenced里程碑计费 · 资金专户

Prepay float is the industry's #1 killer (天道, 太傻). Refund safety becomes our product.预付款浮存是行业头号死因（天道、太傻）。退费安全成为我们的产品。

Genuinely UK-based真正扎根英国

优越 and UKEC sell the story; nobody delivers UK depth at premium price. We are the proof they can't fake.优越和 UKEC 卖的是故事；无人在高端档真实交付英国深度。我们是他们伪造不了的证据。

Zero commissions零院校佣金

"We are paid only by you" — the direct counter to the steering conflict clients now openly discuss.「我们只收你的钱」——正面反制客户已经公开讨论的返佣导向冲突。

Verifiable outcomes可验证的成果

Only 2 of 141 firms audit their numbers. In a market where every figure is assumed false, one honest number wins.141 家中只有 2 家审计自己的数字。在一个所有数字都被默认为假的市场，一个诚实的数字就能赢。

Founder-fronted on 小红书, institutionally documented underneath — multi-destination UK+HK+SG with US/PhD as premium options.小红书上创始人出镜，底层机构化沉淀——英港新多目的地，美国与博士作为高端选项。

08Move 2 · Trust第二步 · 信任

Publish everything competitors hide把对手隐藏的一切都公开

Every Chinese premium boutique hides its prices — and hidden pricing tracks with the industry's #1 complaint class. The Western MBA cluster has published per-school rates for 25 years and compounds quietly. Transparency is a weapon nobody in our tier has picked up.中国高端精品全都藏价——而藏价与行业头号投诉类别如影随形。西方 MBA 集群明码标价 25 年，静静复利。透明是我们档位里没人捡起来的武器。

Price page, verbatim一字不差的价格页

Full ladder + refund policy published on day one. Price filters leads — Menlo proved expensive-and-published converts better buyers.阶梯价格 + 退款政策第一天就全文公开。价格本身筛选客户——Menlo 证明「贵而公开」带来更好的买家。

Annual outcome report年度成果报告

Cohort size, offer rate, first-choice rate, methodology stated — from year one, however small. Only 2 of 141 firms audit outcomes. Audited modest beats claimed perfect.样本量、录取率、首选率、公开方法论——从第一年起，无论多小。141 家中只有 2 家审计成果。经审计的普通数字胜过宣称的完美数字。

Public reviews, no deletions公开评价 · 承诺不删

芝士圈-style per-consultant review ledger with a written no-deletion policy. One deletion accusation is existential for a transparency brand — so make not-deleting a contract.芝士圈式的逐顾问评价账本 + 书面「不删评」政策。对透明品牌而言，一次删评指控就是生死题——所以把「不删」写进承诺。

Never绝不

Self-authored ranking articles (优越), advertorial walls (新通), fantasy statistics ("99%", "98.8%", "zero complaints") — all now recognised and actively held against brands by the buyers we want.自产排名软文（优越）、广告软文墙（新通）、幻想统计（「99%」「98.8%」「零投诉」）——我们想要的那批买家已经识破这些，并把它们记为品牌负分。

09Move 3 · Contract architecture第三步 · 合同架构

Sell the thing families fear losing: their money把家庭最怕失去的东西做成卖点：他们的钱

After 天道 (¥14.23M unrefunded), 太傻 and Valeon, prepay loss is Chinese families' #1 fear. No Chinese premium shop answers it. We answer it structurally:天道（欠退费 1423 万）、太傻、再来人之后，预付款损失是中国家庭的头号恐惧。没有一家中国高端机构回应它。我们从结构上回应：

Milestone billing — 30 / 40 / 30里程碑计费——30 / 40 / 30

Ring-fenced client money客户资金专户

Cash for undelivered milestones sits in a designated account — advertised. The anti-天道 product.未交付里程碑的款项存放于独立账户——并公开宣传。这就是「反天道」产品。

Fast early exits早期快速退出

Defined exit points, no-quibble early-stage refunds resolved in days. Occasional refunds are marketing spend.明确的退出节点，早期无条件退款、数日内到账。偶发退款就是营销费用。

Never guarantees绝不做担保

No 保录取, no "full refund if unsuccessful" — guarantees are debt (太傻) that drifts into fraud (AdmitWrite, in court records). Optional contingent success payment only.不做保录取、不做「不成功全退」——担保是负债（太傻），并会滑向欺诈（AdmitWrite，已入判决书）。至多设或有成功付款。
